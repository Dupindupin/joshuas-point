# House Photography Workspace

This directory is a temporary development workspace for Joshua's Point House photography.

Every asset in this directory is **development photography**, **not final**, and must be
**replaced before production** unless it later completes the separate Sanity approval workflow.

- Do not treat assets placed here as approved or production-ready.
- Each subfolder represents an editorial role in the House page photography plan.
- Final approved images will later move to Sanity, where their metadata, rights, crops, captions,
  and alternative text can be managed.
- Assets may be connected to the frontend only as explicit development photography. Nothing may be
  treated as production media or moved to Sanity until it has completed editorial, factual, rights,
  privacy, and accessibility review.
- The current development mapping uses photography for Hero, Shared Heart, Indoor/Outdoor,
  Pool Relationship, Deck/Roof Relationship, Evening, and three temporary complete-flow placeholders.
- View, Morning, and Rain contain duplicate placeholders for visual testing only. They do not
  document those moments, are not production approved, and must be replaced before launch.
