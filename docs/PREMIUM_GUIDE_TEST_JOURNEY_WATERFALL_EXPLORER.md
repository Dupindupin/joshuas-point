# Joshua's Point — Premium Guide Test Journey 01

## Waterfall Explorer Day from Joshua's Point

**Document type:** Premium Guide production example
**Edition:** Southern Negros Oriental from Joshua's Point — Edition 1
**Journey status:** Editorial pilot; not yet approved for release
**Primary route:** Published `Waterfall Explorer` scenic route
**Primary places:** Valencia, Casaroro Falls, Pulangbato Falls
**Owner source:** Tobias Steger

## Purpose of this test

This is the first complete example of a premium Joshua's Point journey. It tests whether the guide
can turn existing destination pages, a verified scenic route, approved maps, and Tobias's own
observations into one useful day without duplicating the public website.

It is intentionally one journey, not a chapter or a complete guide. Its purpose is to establish a
repeatable quality standard for:

- a clear reason for choosing the day;
- an emotional sequence grounded in real observation;
- a route that begins and ends at Joshua's Point;
- honest practical preparation;
- offline-readable map and photography requirements; and
- a fair boundary between useful free content and premium synthesis.

The pilot succeeds only if it sounds like Tobias sharing a journey he recommends—not a tour
operator selling two attractions.

## Source and authority record

This example uses:

- the published `Waterfall Explorer` scenic route and its owner-approved route/map data;
- the published Casaroro Falls destination;
- the existing Pulangbato Falls and Valencia destination relationships;
- Tobias's approved Casaroro Falls owner-source record;
- the approved **Descent → Gorge → Return** Casaroro narrative;
- the Premium Guide's 13-part journey format; and
- the Joshua's Point editorial rule that observations and changing facts remain distinct.

The document does not introduce a new distance, travel time, fee, opening hour, access promise,
weather promise, or safety claim.

---

## 1. Journey purpose

Offer one considered highland day that shows two different relationships with water and landscape.
Casaroro is experienced through the descent, the gorge, the walk, and the return. Pulangbato brings
a more open setting and mineral-coloured rock. Valencia gives the two experiences a geographic and
editorial connection.

The day is not about collecting two waterfalls. Its value is in the change of landscape: leaving
Joshua's Point, climbing toward the highlands, entering Casaroro's gorge, meeting a different
waterfall setting, and allowing the return to complete the journey.

### What this journey should achieve

The reader should understand:

- why Tobias genuinely recommends Casaroro Falls;
- why the walk and return matter as much as the waterfall;
- how Casaroro and Pulangbato differ without ranking them;
- what the approved route asks of the traveler;
- which details must still be confirmed on the day; and
- why leaving space is more important than adding another stop.

### What this journey must not become

- A “two waterfalls in one day” checklist.
- A timed tour schedule.
- A claim that every guest will find the journey easy, safe, quiet, or rewarding.
- A substitute for current access, weather, or local guidance.
- A sales page for Joshua's Point, transport, or an operator.

---

## 2. Guest profile

### Best suited to

- Guests who want an active nature-led day rather than a sequence of roadside stops.
- Travelers interested in the journey, landscape, and walk—not only the final photograph.
- Guests comfortable making an individual assessment of a long stairway, gorge walk, and return
  climb.
- People who can travel without rushing and are willing to change plans if current conditions make
  that sensible.

### Requires individual judgment

Casaroro includes a long stairway and a walk through the gorge. Tobias remembers that good shoes
were important and that surfaces could be slippery. The published scenic-route guidance also asks
travelers to take particular care when surfaces are wet.

This is not a universal difficulty or safety rating. A traveler must assess their own mobility,
fitness, footwear, current conditions, and advice at the site. The guide must not label the journey
“family-friendly,” “easy,” or suitable for every age or ability without a separate current review.

### Not positioned for

- Guests looking for a low-effort sightseeing stop.
- Anyone who cannot comfortably assess the stairs and return climb.
- Travelers who need guaranteed access, weather, surface, or timing conditions.
- A person relying on the guide as turn-by-turn navigation.

---

## 3. Emotional story

### Working opening

The road leaves Joshua's Point and climbs toward Valencia. The coast gives way to greener highland
roads, and the day begins to feel different before the first waterfall is reached.

Casaroro Falls is not only the moment at the end. Tobias remembers the descent, the walk through
the canyon, the quiet, and the landscape around the waterfall. The journey into the gorge is part
of why he recommends the place. It brings together nature, discovery, adventure, and the happiness
he felt after experiencing it.

Pulangbato changes the atmosphere. Its mineral-coloured rock and more open setting tell a different
story of water in the same highlands. The contrast gives the day its shape without asking one place
to compete with the other.

The return should remain part of the story. A waterfall day from Joshua's Point is not finished at
the last viewpoint; it closes as the road leaves the highlands and leads home again.

### Editorial notes

- “Happiness” belongs to Tobias's memory; do not promise it as the reader's reaction.
- “Quiet” belongs to Tobias's Casaroro observation; do not guarantee crowd levels.
- Do not publish the remembered exact step count or canyon walking time unless separately approved
  as current practical facts.
- Do not add unrecorded sounds, wildlife, weather, geological explanation, or dialogue.
- Keep the tone personal and observant. Avoid “hidden gem,” “must-see,” “breathtaking,” “paradise,”
  and “unforgettable.”

## 4. Route structure

The route sequence is inherited from the approved `Waterfall Explorer` scenic route. The guide
adds day-level editorial context but does not create different geometry or duplicate its canonical
map data.

### Journey sequence

#### 1. Start — Joshua's Point

Begin from Joshua's Point with enough space in the day for the highland journey to unfold without
rushing.

**Guide role:** Emotional and practical origin.
**Map treatment:** Public-safe Joshua's Point area/origin only; no private property coordinate.
**Current confirmation:** Weather, access, transport, and the preferred order of the two falls.

#### 2. Transition — Valencia

Valencia is the doorway into the highlands and a natural pause before continuing toward the
waterfalls.

**Guide role:** Orientation and transition, not a third attraction to complete.
**Map treatment:** Use the verified canonical destination marker and route geometry.
**Current confirmation:** Any planned stop, food, fuel, or other service must be confirmed rather
than assumed.

#### 3. Central experience — Casaroro Falls

The descent, the gorge walk, the waterfall, and the return climb form one experience. The guide
should give this part of the day enough editorial and practical space.

**Guide role:** Primary owner-led story.
**Map treatment:** Use the verified public destination marker; do not add technical trail points
without an approved source.
**Current confirmation:** Access, local rules, fees, opening, weather effects, and surface
conditions.

#### 4. Contrasting experience — Pulangbato Falls

Pulangbato brings a more open waterfall setting and mineral-coloured rock. It belongs because the
contrast makes the highland landscape more understandable, not because the guide is maximizing
the number of stops.

**Guide role:** A second landscape chapter.
**Map treatment:** Use the verified canonical destination marker and route relationship.
**Current confirmation:** Access, local rules, fees, opening, and any activity or facility details.

#### 5. Return — Joshua's Point

Follow the approved route home. The guide should close with the change from the highlands back to
Joshua's Point, not with another destination recommendation.

**Guide role:** Completion and reflection.
**Map treatment:** Return path from the canonical route; public-safe endpoint.
**Current confirmation:** Departure timing appropriate to current conditions.

### Canonical route facts available now

| Item | Approved source value | Premium treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route name | Waterfall Explorer | Retain as the canonical route relationship |
| Route distance | 53.5 km | Read from the route source; do not duplicate as editable guide prose |
| Travel label | Around 1 hour 20 minutes of travel before time spent at the stops | Preserve exactly unless the route source changes |
| Road-quality classification | Paved | Read from the canonical scenic route |
| Route difficulty classification | Demanding | Present with the route's explanation, never as a stand-alone badge |
| Ordered stops | Joshua's Point → Valencia → Casaroro Falls → Pulangbato Falls | Preserve canonical order unless Tobias approves a different day sequence |

The travel label does not define the total length of the day. The guide must not infer a full-day
duration by adding map estimates or unreviewed visit times.

## 5. Included places

### Joshua's Point

**Role:** Starting point, return point, and editorial point of view.
**Premium contribution:** Choosing the day, leaving without rushing, and closing the journey at
home.
**Boundary:** No booking language and no exact private coordinate.

### Valencia

**Role:** Highland orientation and route transition.
**Premium contribution:** Helps the reader understand the change from coast to highlands and the
relationship between the two waterfalls.
**Boundary:** Do not create a Valencia mini-guide or insert unapproved commercial stops.

### Casaroro Falls

**Role:** Primary journey story.
**Approved owner observations:** The landscape, walk, jungle, gorge, waterfall, quiet, descent,
return, adventure, discovery, and Tobias's happiness at the end.
**Boundary:** Exact steps, walking time, conditions, access, fees, hours, and objective suitability
remain outside the journey unless the canonical destination record approves them.

### Pulangbato Falls

**Role:** Contrasting highland waterfall landscape.
**Approved route context:** Mineral-coloured rock and a more open setting.
**Boundary:** Do not add claims about water temperature, pools, swimming, facilities, food, fees,
or access unless present in the current approved destination source.

### Deliberately excluded

- Twin Lakes and other highland destinations.
- Extra cafés, restaurants, shops, viewpoints, or commercial stops without owner selection.
- Detours introduced only to make the day look more valuable.
- Technical trail waypoints not already part of the verified map system.

## 6. Practical information

Premium value comes from organizing what matters, not filling every field. Changing information
must point back to the canonical public pages.

### Approved preparation

- Begin with enough fuel rather than relying on stops near the falls.
- Use established visitor parking and follow current local instructions.
- Wear footwear appropriate for the Casaroro stairway and gorge walk; Tobias remembers that good
  shoes mattered when surfaces were slippery.
- Travel within personal ability and make an individual assessment of the descent and return.
- Leave enough time for the journey without treating the published travel label as a complete-day
  estimate.

### Confirm before leaving

- Current access at Casaroro Falls and Pulangbato Falls.
- Current opening arrangements and local rules.
- Current fees or registration requirements.
- Weather and recent rain.
- Current road and walking-surface conditions.
- Transport arrangements and the preferred waterfall order.
- Any temporary closure or conservation restriction.

### Deliberately absent until supported

- A promised departure or return time.
- Exact visit duration at either waterfall.
- Exact step count or gorge walking time.
- Prices, fees, opening hours, and named transport providers.
- Swimming guidance.
- Universal age, fitness, accessibility, or family-suitability claims.
- Guaranteed crowd levels, quiet, weather, or road conditions.

### Shorter or slower alternative

No alternative should be invented for the pilot. Tobias must decide whether the premium journey
may recommend:

- Casaroro only;
- Pulangbato only; or
- a different highland pause already supported by approved content.

Until that decision is recorded, the guide should say only that travelers may choose one
waterfall when the full route does not suit the day.

### Current image status

The existing Casaroro Falls photograph may support layout development. It remains:

- development photography;
- not production approved; and
- replace before release unless its final crop, credit, and identifiable-person consent are
  approved.

The owner-source record attributes the existing Casaroro photographs to Zei Steger. The release
record must preserve the approved credit and any consent condition. No existing image should be
used to imply that it depicts Pulangbato Falls, the complete route, a particular weather condition,
or an activity it does not show.

### Caption and alt-text standard

- Captions identify the photographed place and why that moment belongs in the sequence.
- Alt text describes visible content, not the emotional claim made by the prose.
- Do not put travel advice, SEO phrases, or unsupported geography into alt text.
- Record photographer, credit wording, consent, crop, and production status before release.

## 8. Map requirements

The journey map must be generated from the existing provider-neutral map architecture and the
canonical `Waterfall Explorer` route. It must not own a second copy of coordinates or route
geometry.

### Required map set

#### Journey overview

- Public-safe Joshua's Point origin.
- Valencia orientation.
- Casaroro Falls marker.
- Pulangbato Falls marker.
- Verified route line.
- Return relationship to Joshua's Point.

#### Detail orientation

- One readable highland inset if it materially improves clarity.
- No technical trail geometry unless separately verified and approved.
- No exact private Joshua's Point coordinate.

#### Accessible text alternative

1. Begin at the public-safe Joshua's Point area.
2. Follow the approved Waterfall Explorer route toward Valencia.
3. Continue to Casaroro Falls.
4. Continue to Pulangbato Falls in the canonical sequence.
5. Return along the approved route to Joshua's Point.

This sequence is orientation, not turn-by-turn navigation.

### Map production requirements

- Read coordinates, ordered stops, and path geometry from canonical records.
- Include map source attribution and the applicable OpenStreetMap-compatible provider attribution.
- Show edition number, map version, and last-reviewed date.
- Include a public canonical route URL or QR code for corrections.
- Design for phone reading and a printable map-pack page.
- Ensure every marker and route relationship is also available as text.
- Do not publish a route line if canonical geometry becomes unavailable.

## 9. Premium-only value

The premium guide does not charge for knowing that Casaroro and Pulangbato exist. It earns value by
turning several public records into a considered, offline-ready day.

### Premium value in this journey

- A deliberate start and return from Joshua's Point.
- Tobias's first-hand reason for recommending Casaroro.
- A complete emotional sequence rather than disconnected place summaries.
- The contrast between gorge and open highland waterfall settings.
- One ordered route with preparation and confirmation points in the right context.
- A quieter one-place alternative once Tobias approves it.
- An offline map sheet generated from verified canonical data.
- A compact day checklist and source/review record.
- Photography arranged to reveal the progression of the journey.
- Saved planning time without hiding essential corrections or access information.

### What is not premium value

- Repeating destination descriptions behind a paywall.
- Withholding current closures, safety information, or conservation rules.
- Adding unverified precision to appear comprehensive.
- More stops, longer lists, or promotional recommendations.
- A claim that the guide replaces current local confirmation.

## 10. Free website boundary

The public platform remains complete and useful.

### Free public content

- The Casaroro Falls destination page and its approved owner-led story.
- The Pulangbato Falls and Valencia destination pages.
- The Waterfall Explorer scenic-route introduction, current canonical route facts, and map.
- Essential preparation, access, safety, conservation, closure, and correction information.
- Public relationships among the route and its destinations.
- Accessible map/list alternatives.
- A representative excerpt or sample from this journey if Tobias approves it.

### Premium content

- The full day-level sequence from Joshua's Point and back.
- Tobias's expanded selection context: why this day, why these places, and why this order.
- Comparative planning between the two waterfall experiences.
- Journey-specific preparation and confirmation logic assembled in one place.
- Approved shorter/slower alternative.
- Offline journey map and printable map-pack page.
- Complete photographic sequence and production captions.
- Edition-specific sources, review date, and compact field reference.

### Boundary test

Removing the premium product must not make the public destination or route pages misleading,
unsafe, or deliberately incomplete. The premium value is synthesis, portability, and Tobias's
considered day—not access to essential facts.

## 13. Template standard established by Journey 01

Future premium journeys should use this pilot's discipline:

1. Start with one clear reason for the day.
2. Reference canonical destinations, routes, dive areas, images, and map data rather than copying
   them.
3. Let Tobias's first-hand observation carry the emotional story.
4. Shape the day as departure, transition, central experience, meaningful contrast or pause, and
   return.
5. Separate enduring observation from changing practical information.
6. Leave unsupported fields empty.
7. Provide an accessible text alternative to every map.
8. Use photography to explain sequence and place, never to fill a layout.
9. Keep essential current information public.
10. Make premium value come from selection, sequence, context, offline use, and saved planning
    time.

This is the production benchmark. It should be reviewed and approved before the remaining premium
journeys are drafted.
