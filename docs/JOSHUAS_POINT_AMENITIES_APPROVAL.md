## Editorial rule

**Story first. Practical clarity second.**

Amenities should help a guest understand how the house and rooms work. They should not turn
Joshua's Point into a hotel feature catalogue. Public wording must stay factual, short, and close to
Tobias's observations. Empty, outdated, or unconfirmed items remain unpublished.

In the tables below, **Yes** identifies an appropriate public location. It does not mean the same
sentence should be repeated on every marked page. The most natural page owns the full detail; other
pages may link to it or use a shorter practical answer.

### Wording limits for confirmed records

- Do not combine the two room capacities into a whole-house maximum until the booking occupancy is
  separately confirmed.
- Do not add pool dimensions, depth, water treatment, safety, or access rules to the approved pool
  wording.
- Do not describe the energy system as off-grid, uninterrupted, self-sufficient, sustainable, or
  low-impact.
- Exact solar, battery, and rainwater capacities remain owner-recorded technical details but should
  enter public practical copy only after their configuration and units are checked.
- The covered deck is owner-confirmed. Its stated three-metre dimension still needs a defined
  measurement axis before public use.
- “Available” does not imply unlimited use, inclusion in every rate, or the absence of operational
  conditions. Do not add those claims until confirmed.
- Do not add internet speeds, transfer routes, laundry terms, scooter eligibility, fuel, insurance,
  safety rules, or charges without separate owner confirmation.

## Placement principles

### House

Tell how connected spaces, the deck, pool, landscape, and house systems shape daily life. Practical
details may support that story, but they should not become a freestanding wall of features.

### Rooms

Keep the confirmed facts close to each suite: capacity, bed, bathroom, and outlook. Add a future
amenity only when it meaningfully distinguishes the room or answers a likely guest question.

### Plan Your Stay

Own operational information here: what is available, what is included, what is conditional, and
what a guest should arrange in advance. Avoid repeating the House narrative.

### FAQ

Use short answers for details that materially affect planning. Link to the owning page where a
longer explanation already exists.

### Contact

Make it clear that practical questions are welcome. Do not reproduce the complete amenity list on
the Contact page; mention only a few useful examples and answer the guest's actual question.

## Approval maintenance

When Tobias confirms a legacy candidate, move it into the confirmed table with an approval date and
final public wording. When availability changes, mark the record inactive rather than silently
leaving stale information public. Sanity implementation should follow this record; this record must
not be inferred from whatever happens to be visible in a frontend component.
