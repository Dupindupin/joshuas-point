# Joshua's Point — Vision

## Purpose

Joshua's Point was created from memories, love, and the bond between Tobias and his wife.

The private story behind those memories does not need to become marketing. The website should instead express the care, warmth, and meaning that shaped the place.

## Mission

Create the most honest digital expression of Joshua's Point.

The website should help travellers imagine slowing down, reconnecting with nature and the people they love, and creating memories that remain long after they leave.

## Guiding statement

> We are not designing for clicks. We are designing for memories.

## Brand personality

Joshua's Point is:

- Curious
- Playful
- Quiet
- Warm
- Adventurous
- Confident
- Humble

## Core principles

1. Nature is the hero.
2. The house frames the landscape rather than competing with it.
3. The experience should feel warm, calm, and welcoming.
4. Every page should tell a meaningful story.
5. Motion should feel natural and restrained.
6. Technology should disappear behind the experience.
7. Guests should feel invited, never pressured.
8. The private family story must always be treated respectfully.
