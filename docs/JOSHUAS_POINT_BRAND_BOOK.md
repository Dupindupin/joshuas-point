# Joshua's Point — Brand Book

**Status:** Permanent editorial and design foundation
**Audience:** Tobias, editors, designers, photographers, developers, and future collaborators

## The central truth

Joshua's Point should always feel like **Tobias quietly sharing a place he genuinely knows and
loves**.

It must never sound like a hotel selling an experience.

## Mission

Create the most honest digital expression of Joshua's Point: a home connected to nature and a
trusted, personal way into Southern Negros. Help people imagine slowing down, being together, and
discovering the region with care and confidence.

## Vision

Joshua's Point becomes the most trusted personal guide to Southern Negros because it combines a
real place, first-hand knowledge, truthful photography, restrained design, and useful editorial
guidance. Growth is measured by trust and depth, not the number of pages published.

## Philosophy

### Nature is the hero

The sea, Mount Talinis, islands, weather, bamboo, rain, light, wind, and daily rhythms lead the
story. Architecture and interface frame those experiences; they do not compete with them.

### A home, not a product

Joshua's Point is homy, connected to nature, and peaceful. The public experience should feel lived
in and welcoming, never staged as a status object or converted into an anonymous accommodation
product.

### The house frames the landscape

Openings, the covered deck, connected living spaces, and the relationship between inside and
outside help people notice the place. The story is not size, spectacle, or amenity count.

### Discovery happens slowly

Destinations, routes, and dive guides help guests understand a place and its journey. They do not
encourage attraction collecting, rankings, or hurried itineraries.

### Technology disappears

CMS, maps, navigation, motion, themes, SEO, and forms exist to make the story clearer and the guest
journey easier. Visitors should remember the place, not the interface.

## Brand personality

Joshua's Point is:

- Personal and observant.
- Warm and homy.
- Quiet and peaceful.
- Curious and gently adventurous.
- Architectural without becoming formal.
- Confident without boasting.
- Honest and humble.
- Timeless rather than trend-led.

When uncertain, choose the calmer, truer, more useful expression.

## Writing principles

### The voice

Write like Tobias speaking to someone he would genuinely like to help:

- Use simple, natural English.
- Preserve Tobias's meaning and useful structure.
- Improve grammar, clarity, pacing, and transitions without replacing his observations.
- Prefer first-hand detail: coffee outside, birds in the morning, bamboo in the wind, rain on the
  covered deck, warm evening air, and the journey into a place.
- Be quietly enthusiastic when Tobias is genuinely enthusiastic.
- Separate personal observation from verified practical fact.
- End before the writing starts to persuade.

### What the writing avoids

- Hotel and resort language.
- Generic luxury, exclusivity, status, or scarcity.
- “Paradise,” “hidden gem,” “world-class,” “breathtaking,” and “unforgettable experience.”
- Clickbait, listicles, keyword stuffing, rankings, and unsupported superlatives.
- Polished but empty AI-sounding paragraphs.
- Invented memories, quotes, sensations, facts, access details, or technical guidance.
- Private family history or the private meaning behind the Joshua name.

### Calls to action

Actions should be useful and unhurried: Explore the House, View the Rooms, Plan Your Stay, Read the
Story. Never create artificial urgency or pressure.

## Editorial principles

1. Existing Joshua's Point material is the primary migration source.
2. Tobias is the authority for destinations, routes, photographs, recommendations, and lived
   experience.
3. Existing content is improved, not rewritten merely to make it different.
4. Facts remain separate from observation and are dated or sourced when volatile.
5. Unknown optional information disappears; it is never filled with a guess.
6. Original local knowledge matters more than content volume.
7. Relationships are curated because they help the reader, not to increase clicks.
8. Sensitive ecological, cultural, community, and private information is protected.
9. AI may help organize, proofread, compare, or structure source material; it may not invent facts
   or replace Tobias's knowledge.
10. Every published page must remain coherent without marketing claims, a gallery, or related links.

The migration method is:

> Existing website → improve → structure in Sanity → review → publish

## Photography principles

Photography answers: **What would it feel like to be here?**

- Nature, architecture, people, and light are the four central subjects.
- Show relationships: house and land, room and view, deck and weather, people and shared space.
- Use real moments, never fake models or invented activity.
- Preserve natural color. Do not globally darken, tint, recolor, oversaturate, or apply harsh HDR.
- Use negative space, natural light, truthful scale, and uncluttered compositions.
- Captions provide context; alt text communicates the image's purpose; credits and rights remain
  attached to the asset.
- Never represent a photograph as another place, weather condition, room, route, or experience.
- Drone work should show the relationship between home and landscape, not altitude for spectacle.

Existing Joshua's Point photography is owner photography unless Tobias says otherwise. During
development, temporary assets must remain clearly identified as **development photography**, **not
production approved**, and **replace before launch**. Replacement happens through Sanity without a
frontend redesign.

## Motion principles

Motion should feel like wind, drifting cloud, water, and a gradual change in light.

- Use subtle fade, short movement, and at most a very small scale change.
- Interface feedback is brief; editorial reveals are slower and quieter.
- Motion supports hierarchy and orientation. It never delays access to content.
- Never bounce, flash, spin, shake, use dramatic parallax, or make large zooms.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; the complete experience must work without animation.
- Theme changes should be short and gentle, without a wrong-theme flash.

Visitors should remember the place, not the animation.

## Color philosophy

The palette comes from the place:

- Warm linen and cream for morning canvas.
- Sand and stone for quiet surfaces and borders.
- Charcoal for ink and structure.
- Forest and timber for restrained warmth and action.
- Ocean as a quiet secondary note.
- Deep warm charcoal, muted forest, and deep ocean for evening surfaces.
- Warm off-white and restrained timber/gold warmth in Dark mode.

White is never pure white. Black is never pure black. Dark mode is an evening atmosphere, not an
inversion. Nature and photography remain more colorful than the interface.

Components use semantic roles—canvas, surface, ink, border, accent, focus, inverse, map, and
warning—rather than raw page-specific colors.

## Typography

- **Display:** Newsreader.
- **Body and interface:** Manrope.

Newsreader carries hero headings, section headings, editorial titles, pull quotes, and large
storytelling moments. Manrope carries paragraphs, navigation, forms, buttons, captions, and UI.

Typography should support the story rather than compete with it. Use generous line height, calm
scale, readable measure, and only restrained uppercase eyebrow/navigation labels. Never introduce a
third brand font or trend-led decorative typography.

## The role of every public area

### Home — the invitation

Joshua's Point comes first, then the nature around it, then Southern Negros. Home introduces the
world without trying to summarize it.

### The House — how living there feels

The House explains the relationship between home and land: shared spaces, deck, view, weather,
materials, inside and outside, and the rhythms of morning, rain, and evening.

### Rooms — clear, warm accommodation information

Rooms answers practical questions about Ocean Suite and Garden Suite without turning them into
e-commerce inventory or inventing qualities that have not been confirmed.

### Destinations — places Tobias recommends

The destination hub opens the regional world. Individual pages preserve Tobias's observations,
make facts useful, and let the journey matter as much as the endpoint.

### Scenic Routes — the pleasure of moving through the landscape

Routes are not merely directions. They show coast, highlands, stops, and changing character while
keeping verified maps and practical orientation available.

### Guide — the wider editorial doorway

The Southern Negros Guide connects destinations, diving, routes, food, coffee, and practical
knowledge without becoming a catalogue or sales page.

### Dive Guides — grounded local orientation

Dive pages introduce areas and stories while withholding technical conditions until qualified
review. They never replace an operator briefing, current conditions, training, or professional
judgment.

### Plan Your Stay — reassurance

This page communicates confirmed policies and expectations in plain language. It does not hide
unknown conditions behind hospitality wording.

### Getting Here, FAQ, and Contact — human help

These pages remove uncertainty, state what is known, and offer a real conversation. The enquiry form
is not a booking engine and receipt is not availability confirmation.

## The visitor's emotional journey

1. **Arrival:** Home creates quiet attention and introduces Joshua's Point itself.
2. **Recognition:** The House helps the visitor imagine ordinary life there.
3. **Practical confidence:** Rooms answers where people sleep without disrupting the atmosphere.
4. **Curiosity:** Destinations, routes, Guide, and Dive pages open Southern Negros slowly.
5. **Trust:** Specific observations, honest omissions, maps, captions, and reviewed facts show care.
6. **Reassurance:** Getting Here, FAQ, and Plan Your Stay reduce uncertainty.
7. **Conversation:** Contact feels like writing to a thoughtful host, not entering a sales funnel.
8. **Quiet close:** The Footer returns the visitor to Joshua's Point as the place from which the
   wider region is discovered.

## What Joshua's Point must never become

- A hotel, resort, or luxury-status brand.
- A party venue or a promise of unrestricted use.
- A property brochure obsessed with size, amenities, or architectural prestige.
- A generic travel portal, attraction catalogue, content farm, or rankings site.
- An SEO-first publication that repeats place names instead of earning attention.
- A marketplace for operators, sponsored placement, or disguised advertising.
- A booking funnel built around urgency, scarcity, pop-ups, and aggressive conversion.
- A design showcase where motion, typography, themes, or maps overwhelm photography and story.
- A collection of AI-written memories or unverified practical claims.
- A public archive of private family meaning.
- A platform that grows faster than Tobias can keep truthful, useful, and cared for.

## The final test

Before publishing a word, photograph, interaction, or feature, ask:

1. Is it true?
2. Is it useful?
3. Does it protect privacy and place?
4. Does it sound like Tobias?
5. Does it leave room for nature?

If not, remove it or return to the source.
