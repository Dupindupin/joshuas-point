# Joshua's Point — Southern Negros Premium Guide Owner Decision Record

## Status and purpose

This document records the 25 owner decisions required before the Southern Negros Premium Guide
content schemas and production workflow are implemented. It is based on:

- `SOUTHERN_NEGROS_PREMIUM_GUIDE_PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md`
- `SOUTHERN_NEGROS_PREMIUM_GUIDE_EDITORIAL_BLUEPRINT.md`
- `SOUTHERN_NEGROS_PREMIUM_GUIDE_CONTENT_MODEL.md`

No decision marked **Pending** is approved merely because a recommendation appears here. Tobias's
recorded choice becomes the authority.

## How to use this record

For each item, record:

- **Owner decision:** the approved choice in Tobias's words.
- **Decision date:** when it was approved.
- **Notes or conditions:** any boundary that future editors or developers must preserve.

When a decision changes, retain the previous choice in Git history and update the edition or
implementation impact explicitly.

## 1. Product decisions

### Decision 1 — Product name and subtitle

**Why it matters**

The name defines the geographic promise and the relationship with Joshua's Point. It must not imply
that Edition 1 covers all of Negros or Siquijor.

**Recommended option**

Use **Southern Negros Oriental from Joshua's Point** as the Edition 1 title. Develop a short,
descriptive subtitle only after the manuscript direction is approved; avoid “ultimate,” “complete,”
and sales language.

**Owner choice needed**

- Approve or replace the title.
- Approve the subtitle direction.
- Confirm whether “Joshua's Point” should appear in every future edition name.

## 4. Technical decisions

### Decision 21 — PDF generation approach

**Why it matters**

The generation method determines layout control, accessibility, repeatability, versioning, and how
easily Edition 1 can become Edition 1.1 or Edition 2.

**Recommended option**

Generate the publication from a typed, versioned guide export rather than manually copying text
from Sanity. Use a controlled editorial layout source suitable for print and small screens, then
tag, remediate, and validate the final PDF for accessibility. Record the exact Sanity revisions and
file checksum in the release manifest.

**Owner choice needed**

- Approve automated/repeatable generation rather than a manually maintained final file.
- Decide whether final visual layout is produced in code, a professional publishing application,
  or a controlled hybrid workflow.
- Approve the required accessibility standard and review responsibility.
