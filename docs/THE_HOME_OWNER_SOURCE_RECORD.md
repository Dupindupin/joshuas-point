## Purpose

This record gathers the source material currently approved for the Joshua's Point Home page. It
does not write final copy. Its purpose is to keep editorial direction, owner observation, verified
fact, and private information separate before photography, Sanity, or frontend implementation.

The Home hierarchy approved by the owner is:

```text
Joshua's Point as the emotional center
→ nature around it
→ regional discoveries in Southern Negros
```

The Home page must feel **Homy**, **Connected to nature**, and **Peaceful**. Joshua's Point comes
first. The region is the surrounding world, not a tourism catalogue placed ahead of the home.

---

## Source status vocabulary

| Status                          | Meaning                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner-approved direction**    | The owner has approved the subject and editorial role, but not necessarily a factual sentence or final wording.      |
| **Owner-confirmed observation** | The owner has recorded a lived, public-safe observation. It may support writing within its stated limits.            |
| **Owner-confirmed fact**        | The owner has confirmed the fact; technical, geographic, legal, or asset-specific use may still require evidence.    |
| **Project principle**           | Approved Joshua's Point philosophy. It guides writing but is not proof of a physical fact.                           |
| **Platform principle**          | Approved direction for the Southern Negros editorial guide. It does not verify a particular destination or activity. |
| **Missing source**              | No approved observation or fact exists yet. Leave the public field unwritten.                                        |
| **Excluded**                    | Known or potentially available information that must remain private or outside the Home workflow.                    |

### Source rule

An owner-approved topic is not permission to invent a scene. For example, “cooking together” is
approved Shared Life territory; it does not establish what was cooked, who was present, when it
happened, or how anyone felt.
