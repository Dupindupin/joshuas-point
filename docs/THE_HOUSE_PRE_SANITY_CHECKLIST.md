# Joshua's Point — The House Pre-Sanity Checklist

**Document type:** Internal frontend and content readiness gate
**Route supported:** `/the-house`
**Status:** Pre-integration audit; CMS implementation not approved by this document
**Public website copy:** No

## Purpose

This checklist determines what must be complete before the House page moves from typed local data
to Sanity. It consolidates:

- `JOSHUA_POINT_HOUSE_SOUL.md`
- `THE_HOUSE_CONTENT_MAP.md`
- `THE_HOUSE_VISUAL_QA.md`

It separates four kinds of readiness that must not be confused:

1. Approved editorial principles.
2. Approved public content.
3. Development photography and layout.
4. CMS-ready structured data.

The House page is structurally ready for continued development. It is not yet ready for final
content migration. Sanity should manage truthful, approved material—not become a place where
editors are encouraged to fill unresolved sections with plausible language.

## Readiness vocabulary

| Status              | Meaning                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Owner-confirmed** | Recorded directly from Tobias Steger as source; technical or publication checks may remain. |

| **Blocked**         | Required source, verification, photography, rights, privacy, or visual QA is missing.       |

## Modelling principle

Use one explicit `housePage` singleton with named section fields. Do not introduce a generic page
builder or unrestricted `sections[]` array. Reuse the project's existing editorial image, SEO, and
workflow objects where their approved fields already meet the House requirements.

Only public, approved content belongs in the document. Source interviews, private memories,
verification evidence, rights agreements, consent records, and sensitive location information need
a separate restricted workflow.

## Validation rules

### Document-level

- Treat `housePage` as a singleton and restrict duplicate creation and deletion through the existing
  Studio singleton pattern.
- Require the minimum approved Hero, Opening Reflection, Shared Heart, View, Indoor/Outdoor, and
  Closing data before the document is considered publishable.
- Reuse the existing workflow and review-date conventions; do not invent a parallel House-only
  status system.
- Show non-blocking editorial warnings for missing SEO, hero photography, image credit, gallery or
  sequence completeness, and review date where supported by the existing Studio workflow.

### Text

- Trim whitespace and reject empty required strings.
- Keep Opening Reflection and Closing Reflection constrained to their approved short forms.
- Do not impose arbitrary character limits until approved public writing establishes realistic
  editorial bounds.
- Do not accept internal verification notes or private source material in public text fields.

### Images

- Require an asset for every retained image record.
- Require meaningful alt text for editorially informative House images.
- Keep captions optional; never require filler caption text.
- Require or warn for photographer credit according to the existing shared image policy.
- Ensure crop and hotspot or focal information remain compatible with desktop and mobile rendering.
- Rights and consent evidence must be complete before publication even if stored outside the public
  image object.

### Indoor/Outdoor

- Restrict roles to the approved small vocabulary.
- Prevent duplicate roles within one image sequence.
- Require at least two complete images for a publishable sequence; do not require a third solely for
  symmetry.
- Reject incomplete image objects.

### Daily Rhythms

- Restrict moments to Morning, Rain, and Evening.
- Prevent duplicate moment types.
- Allow incomplete moments to be omitted.
- Require image and approved observation together for every retained moment.
- Recommend no more than three moments.

### Materials and Architecture

- Omit the whole object when no material story is confirmed.
- Require at least one complete material when the object is present.
- Keep the list ordered and recommend no more than six entries.
- Require `name` and `description` for every retained entry.
- Never use validation defaults to turn provisional Timber, Stone, Light, or Air records into public
  facts.
- Do not treat owner-confirmed presence as sufficient for technical terminology, performance, or
  sustainability claims; require the relevant supporting record before publication.

## Query and mapping requirements

- Create one centralized typed GROQ query for the House singleton.
- Keep GROQ out of section components and `page.tsx`.
- Map the query result into the existing presentation contract.
- Omit incomplete sections and moments gracefully.
- Do not expose internal workflow or restricted source fields in public query projections.
- Map Sanity image data through the existing shared image utility into `EditorialMedia` props.
- Preload only the approved Hero image.
- Preserve current metadata behavior with approved SEO fields and a safe code fallback.
- Keep the route server-rendered; no client component is required for CMS integration.

The frontend architecture is ready for a typed CMS integration. The public content and photography
are not yet ready to migrate.

Owner verification has materially advanced geography, architectural authorship and intent,
materials, deck and pool relationships, house systems, and guest-experience observations. The next
approval gate is reached when the remaining owner source, documentary and terminology checks, the
missing View, Morning, and Rain photographs, source originals, rights, responsive visual QA, and
field-ownership decisions are complete. Until then, `HousePageData` and the separate
development-photography map should remain the working presentation contract.
