# Joshua's Point — Southern Negros Premium Guide Product Definition

## Status and scope

This document defines the proposed premium product based on the approved
`SOUTHERN_NEGROS_ORIENTAL_GUIDE_SCOPE.md`. It does not create content, approve legacy facts, select a
payment provider, build checkout, modify the frontend, or change Sanity.

The working product name is **Southern Negros Oriental from Joshua's Point**. The final title remains
an owner decision.

## Product definition at a glance

The premium guide is an offline-ready editorial field companion for discovering Southern Negros
Oriental from Joshua's Point. It turns Tobias's experience, tested journeys, selected places,
practical preparation, and verified map information into a coherent publication that is easier to
use than dozens of separate web pages.

It is not a larger destination directory and not a paid version of information that should remain
public. Its value comes from selection, sequence, context, portability, and saved planning time.

### Recommended Version 1 package

- One accessible, mobile-readable PDF edition.
- A small downloadable map pack designed for screen and print.
- A public web companion for current destination pages, corrections, and changing information.
- A clearly numbered edition with publication date and last-reviewed date.
- Optional EPUB only after the chapter and map experience has been tested successfully.

## 1. Product promise

### What someone receives

A buyer receives a considered guide for turning a stay at Joshua's Point into meaningful days
across Southern Negros Oriental.

The guide brings together:

- Tobias's personal reasons for recommending each place and journey.
- Curated days leaving from and returning to Joshua's Point.
- A practical arrival path through Dumaguete.
- Selected destinations across Zamboanguita, Dauin, Valencia, Siaton, and Apo Island.
- Owner-approved scenic routes and the stops that belong naturally along them.
- Editorial diving orientation for Dauin and Apo Island, with clear technical boundaries.
- Preparation notes, useful local context, and reminders about what must be confirmed.
- Offline-readable maps and text directions based on verified location data.
- A compact field-notes section for supplies, cash, connectivity, transport, and other practical
  needs.
- A dated corrections and update path through the public Joshua's Point platform.

### The promise in one sentence

> A personal, offline-ready guide to choosing and planning the Southern Negros Oriental journeys
> Tobias would share from Joshua's Point.

### The outcome for the buyer

After using the guide, a reader should be able to:

1. Choose a day that fits their interests, available time, and preparation.
2. Understand why the journey is worth taking from Joshua's Point.
3. See how the road, stops, destination, and return fit together.
4. Know what to bring and which changing details to confirm locally.
5. Keep essential orientation available when mobile signal is weak.
6. Spend less time comparing fragmented sources and more time understanding the place.

### What the product does not promise

- Real-time road, weather, sea, transport, fee, opening, or access information.
- Guaranteed wildlife, marine life, visibility, or dive conditions.
- Turn-by-turn navigation.
- Technical diving instruction or a substitute for a qualified local briefing.
- Suitability or accessibility without current individual assessment.
- A complete directory of Southern Negros Oriental.
- Discounts, bookings, availability, or preferential access.

## 2. Audience

### Joshua's Point guests

The primary audience. They want to understand what belongs in a day from the house without opening
many tabs or sorting through a regional tourism directory.

The product should help them:

- Plan before arrival.
- Choose between coast, island, waterfall, lake, mountain, diving, or a slower local day.
- Leave Joshua's Point prepared and return without making the day feel rushed.
- Understand Tobias's personal relationship with the recommended places.

### Independent travelers

Travelers who are not staying at Joshua's Point but value the same pace and perspective. Joshua's
Point remains the guide's center, giving the product a real point of view rather than pretending to
be universally neutral.

The product should help them:

- Understand the southern region as a connected landscape.
- Use Dumaguete as a gateway without making the city the whole journey.
- Build a small number of coherent days rather than collect attractions.
- Find the canonical public pages when current information is needed.

### Divers

Divers interested in Dauin, Apo Island, and the southern coast who need regional context before
speaking with a qualified operator.

The product may provide:

- The relationship between dive areas, coast, island, community, and nearby journeys.
- Conservation and respectful-travel context.
- Photography and preparation observations where approved.
- Questions to confirm with a qualified operator.

It must not function as a technical dive planner. Depth, current, visibility, level, conditions,
access, and safety require qualified review and current local confirmation.

### Families

Families choosing days with different energy levels, interests, and practical needs.

The guide should provide honest decision support:

- The general shape and effort of a journey.
- What preparation may be needed.
- Where a shorter or quieter alternative exists.
- Which suitability questions must be answered directly by the family or a current local source.

It should not label an activity “family-friendly” without approved evidence or imply that one
family's experience applies to every child or adult.

### Nature travelers

Readers drawn to forest, waterfalls, lakes, highlands, coast, island ecosystems, and the changing
relationship between sea and mountain.

The product should help them understand:

- Why each place belongs in the region's larger story.
- How light, weather, road, and pace shape the experience.
- How to visit with care and respect.
- When leaving space in the day matters more than adding another stop.

### Who the guide is not primarily for

- Travelers seeking an exhaustive directory or ranked “top attractions” list.
- Buyers looking for live prices, availability, or bookings.
- People expecting technical dive instruction or guaranteed conditions.
- Anyone wanting nightlife, resort comparisons, or promotional deals as the organizing principle.

## 3. Free versus Premium boundary

The free and paid products must strengthen one another. The public platform earns trust and keeps
current information accessible. The premium guide saves time and makes deeper knowledge portable.

### Free public platform

The free website should provide:

- Inspiration and a clear introduction to Southern Negros Oriental.
- Selected full stories that demonstrate the real editorial quality.
- Basic destination and area information.
- Why Tobias recommends a place where approved owner source exists.
- Essential access, conservation, conduct, closure, and safety-related updates.
- Canonical destination, scenic-route, and dive-guide pages.
- The Explorer Map and accessible text listings.
- Basic relationships between places and journeys.
- Public corrections and current last-reviewed information.

Free content should be genuinely useful. It must not be deliberately weakened to make the premium
guide necessary.

### Premium guide

The paid product should provide:

- Curated journeys assembled across several canonical places.
- Tested day shapes beginning from Joshua's Point.
- Ordered itineraries with pace, stops, preparation, and alternatives.
- Deeper planning knowledge that reduces fragmented research.
- Tobias's personal recommendations and observed reasons for choosing one journey over another.
- Offline-readable chapters, maps, indexes, and practical references.
- Comparative guidance: coast or highlands, shorter or longer day, active or quiet journey.
- Print-quality editorial sequencing and photography.
- Edition-level review dates, source notes, and a clear corrections path.

### Boundary rules

| Information | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place introduction and why it matters | Complete, concise | Connected to a deeper regional chapter |
| Essential access or safety correction | Always | Repeated with edition date and public update link |
| Individual destination map | Public interactive/text map | Offline editorial map and cross-reference |
| Basic practical information | Public where necessary | Synthesized into preparation and field notes |
| Owner observation | Representative and authentic | Deeper context across a complete journey |
| Scenic route | Public route introduction and current canonical facts | Day sequence, pauses, preparation, alternatives, offline map |
| Itinerary | A representative sample may be public | Curated set designed as the main paid utility |
| Photography | Selected public photography | Publication sequence, map context, and print-quality edit |
| Corrections | Always public | Linked from every edition |

### Information that must never be paywalled

- Known closures and material access changes.
- Conservation restrictions.
- Material corrections to previously published advice.
- Safety-related warnings.
- Emergency and public-health information.
- The limitations of dive and route guidance.

## 4. Product format

### PDF

**Role:** Recommended primary format for Version 1.

**Strengths**

- Preserves editorial composition, photography, maps, and cross-references.
- Works offline on phones, tablets, laptops, and common document readers.
- Can be made printable and accessible.
- Straightforward to version, watermark lightly if required, and deliver securely.

**Limitations**

- Fixed layouts require careful testing on small screens.
- Larger photography can increase file size.
- Updates require a new file and clear edition handling.

**Recommendation**

Create one accessible portrait PDF designed first for a phone or small tablet while remaining
reasonable to print. Use tagged headings, bookmarks, readable type, meaningful link text, image
descriptions, and a logical reading order. Do not launch separate screen and print editions unless
testing proves one file cannot serve both purposes.

### EPUB

**Role:** Valuable optional reading format, but not a Version 1 launch requirement.

**Strengths**

- Reflowable text and reader-controlled type size.
- Better long-form reading on e-readers and accessibility tools.
- Smaller download size for text-led chapters.

**Limitations**

- Complex maps, spreads, captions, and image sequences are less predictable.
- Requires separate device testing and quality control.
- Can create launch delay without improving field navigation.

**Recommendation**

Build EPUB after the PDF content and structure are stable. Include it in Edition 1 only if the
actual chapter, map, and navigation experience passes testing on common reading applications.

### Downloadable maps

**Role:** Core premium companion, separate from the main PDF where useful.

Recommended map assets:

- Southern Negros Oriental orientation map.
- Joshua's Point-to-Dumaguete gateway map.
- Zamboanguita, Dauin, Valencia, and Siaton area sheets.
- Apo Island journey overview.
- Scenic-route sheets using owner-approved geometry.

Each map should include:

- A text route or location alternative.
- Source and attribution.
- Edition/review date.
- Clear distinction between orientation and navigation.
- A link or QR code to the current public page.

Do not launch offline interactive tiles, GPX downloads, or technical dive coordinates in Version 1.
Those require separate support, versioning, privacy, and safety decisions.

### Web companion

**Role:** The current and connected layer of the product.

The web companion should use the existing public Joshua's Point platform for:

- Canonical destination, route, and dive pages.
- Current corrections and material changes.
- Interactive Explorer Map.
- Related places and onward reading.
- Product support and download recovery when commerce is implemented.

Do not build a duplicate premium website containing copied destination facts. Premium web chapters
may be considered later only if they add a genuine reading or update benefit.

### Future updates

Every product file should carry:

- Product title.
- Edition number.
- Publication date.
- Last reviewed date.
- Canonical corrections/support URL.
- Copyright and license.

Use explicit versioning:

- Major editorial edition: `2.0`
- Material content update: `1.1`
- Correction release: `1.0.1`

Do not promise lifetime updates. Tobias must approve what update period or edition entitlement is
included with a purchase before checkout opens.

| Downloadable PDF map pack | Required | Offline orientation and route reference |

## 5. Edition 1 structure

### Front matter

- A note from Tobias.
- What this guide covers and deliberately leaves out.
- How to use the guide from Joshua's Point or independently.
- Edition, review, limitations, attribution, and corrections information.
- A visual orientation to sea, mountain, coast, and gateway.

### Chapter 1 — From Joshua's Point

**Purpose:** Establish the house as the point of view rather than merely a lodging address.

Sections:

- Reading the day from the deck: landscape, weather, and pace.
- Choosing one meaningful journey.
- Leaving and returning to Joshua's Point.
- Guide symbols, map conventions, and confirmation notes.

### Chapter 2 — Arriving through Dumaguete

**Purpose:** Reduce uncertainty between arrival and the southern coast.

Sections:

- Airport and port orientation.
- A restrained Dumaguete city introduction.
- Food, cash, supplies, mobile data, and other approved practical stops.
- Continuing south toward Joshua's Point.
- What must be reconfirmed close to travel.

### Chapter 3 — Zamboanguita and the Apo Island coast

**Purpose:** Give the immediate area around Joshua's Point its own identity.

Sections:

- Calango and Zamboanguita through approved owner observations.
- Malatapay Market.
- The coast and island-facing landscape.
- Apo Island gateway orientation.
- A quiet nearby day when a longer journey is unnecessary.

### Chapter 4 — Dauin and the marine coast

**Purpose:** Connect coastal life, marine conservation, diving, food, and useful preparation.

Sections:

- Dauin orientation.
- Dauin Marine Sanctuaries.
- Diving and snorkeling context.
- Responsible marine encounters.
- Owner-approved food and useful stops.
- Practical field notes for the coast.

### Chapter 5 — Valencia and the highlands

**Purpose:** Show how quickly the landscape changes from coast to forest, waterfalls, and mountain.

Sections:

- Valencia orientation.
- Casaroro Falls.
- Pulangbato Falls.
- Forest Camp if approved.
- Baslay Hot Spring after access review.
- Mount Talinis as a specialist journey with qualified preparation boundaries.
- A shorter and a fuller highland day.

### Chapter 6 — Siaton: lake, forest, and sheltered coast

**Purpose:** Present Siaton as a journey between mountain and sea rather than isolated attractions.

Sections:

- Siaton orientation.
- Lake Balanan.
- Tambobo Bay.
- The road and landscape between them.
- A quiet nature day and a longer scenic day.

### Chapter 7 — Apo Island

**Purpose:** Give the island journey enough space to include travel, community, conservation, and
the sea.

Sections:

- Why Tobias recommends the journey.
- Leaving Joshua's Point and reaching the gateway.
- What the island experience can include without promising conditions or wildlife.
- Conservation and respectful behavior.
- Diving and snorkeling questions to confirm locally.
- Returning to Joshua's Point.

### Chapter 8 — Roads worth taking

**Purpose:** Make the journey itself part of the product's value.

For each approved route:

- Why take this road.
- The character and rhythm of the journey.
- Ordered stops and meaningful pauses.
- Preparation and known limitations.
- Shorter alternatives and reasons to turn back.
- Verified map and text sequence.
- Related destinations and public update links.

### Chapter 9 — Practical field notes

**Purpose:** Keep useful preparation together without turning the guide into a commercial
directory.

Sections:

- Cash and payments.
- Connectivity and offline preparation.
- Fuel and transport.
- Markets, groceries, and supplies.
- Health and emergency orientation.
- Weather and changing conditions.
- Responsible travel and local respect.
- What to verify on the day.

### Back matter

- Map index.
- Destination and route index.
- Sources, contributors, and photography credits.
- Review-date register.
- Corrections and support URL.
- License and permitted use.

## 6. What makes it worth paying for

### Tobias's experience

The guide is valuable because a real person has chosen what belongs. Tobias's observations explain
why a place fits the Joshua's Point experience, which road he would take, what part of the journey
matters, and when a quieter alternative may be better.

The premium product must clearly distinguish:

- Tobias's first-hand observation.
- Owner recommendation.
- Verified practical fact.
- Qualified specialist guidance.
- Information that can change and must be checked.

### Tested journeys, not assembled lists

The paid unit is the **journey**, not the destination entry.

A strong journey connects:

- The reason for leaving Joshua's Point that day.
- The road and useful pauses.
- The central place or experience.
- Preparation and realistic limitations.
- An alternative if the day, weather, or energy changes.
- The return to Joshua's Point.

This sequencing is difficult to gain from search results or disconnected attraction pages.

### Local knowledge with a point of view

The guide should tell readers which details actually affect a day:

- Where the journey begins in practice.
- Which stops naturally belong together.
- What to organize before leaving.
- What is worth carrying offline.
- Which local detail is useful and which is unnecessary noise.
- What Tobias would choose when there is not time for everything.

### Saved planning time

The guide replaces fragmented research with a small number of trustworthy decisions. It should
reduce the need to compare many tabs, duplicate map searches, disconnected social posts, and
generic lists.

The buyer is paying for:

- Selection.
- Verification and review discipline.
- Clear sequence.
- Honest limits.
- Offline availability.
- Maps designed around the journey.
- A maintained relationship to current public information.

### Editorial and visual coherence

Photography, writing, map design, captions, and practical notes should work as one publication.
The visual experience should carry the same calm, warm, nature-connected identity as Joshua's
Point without becoming a property brochure.

### What does not create paid value

- A larger number of entries.
- Generic “top things to do” lists.
- Copied destination descriptions.
- Artificially withheld basic information.
- Unverified insider claims.
- Hotel, restaurant, or operator rankings.
- Affiliate links or paid placement presented as recommendation.
- Features that require constant connectivity but are marketed as offline.

## 7. Product quality requirements

The guide should not be offered for sale until:

- Every included journey has an identified owner source and editor.
- Canonical place facts are reviewed and dated.
- Technical dive material has qualified review or is omitted.
- Maps use verified public coordinates and approved route geometry.
- No private Joshua's Point coordinate is exposed.
- Photography is approved, credited, and suitable for publication.
- The PDF passes mobile, tablet, print, keyboard, screen-reader, and link testing.
- Download size and offline behavior are tested on realistic connections and devices.
- Corrections, support, licensing, refund, delivery, and update rules are approved.
- A complete real purchase and download test succeeds after commerce is implemented.

### Product success test

Before launch, give the finished guide to representative readers and ask them to plan one day
without additional explanation. The product succeeds when they can:

- Choose an appropriate journey.
- Explain why they chose it.
- Identify what to prepare and confirm.
- Find the map and related practical notes.
- Understand the guide's limitations.
- Return to the current public page when information may have changed.

## Final product statement

The Southern Negros premium guide is not valuable because it contains more places than the free
website. It is valuable because Tobias has connected the right places into days that make sense
from Joshua's Point.

The reader receives a calmer way to plan: fewer choices, clearer journeys, honest preparation,
offline orientation, and the feeling that someone who knows the region has quietly shown them where
to begin.
