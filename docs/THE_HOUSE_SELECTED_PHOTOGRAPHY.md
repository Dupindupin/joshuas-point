## Purpose

This board records the first editorial review of the House photographs currently available in
`web/public/images/house/`. It applies the roles in `THE_HOUSE_PHOTOGRAPHY_BRIEF.md` and the evidence
requirements in `THE_HOUSE_VERIFICATION_RECORD.md`.

The review covers visual content, composition, orientation, current dimensions, and likely
editorial role. The repository does not contain recorded photographer credits, rights agreements,
capture dates, capture times, verified viewpoints, material identification, consent records, or
source-original delivery information. No asset can therefore move to **selected** yet.

Five photographs appear in more than one editorial folder. They are recorded once below as unique
images, with every repository location noted. Duplicate placement does not create a separate asset
or approval. Three duplicates temporarily fill View, Morning, and Rain for complete-flow visual
testing; they do not satisfy those photography briefs.
