## Development status

Every image in this document is:

- **development photography**;
- **not production approved**;
- **replace before launch**.

Selection for a Home role does not make an image final, approve its rights, verify its capture
context, or transfer its House-page role to Home permanently. Final images will later be managed
through Sanity and must remain replaceable without redesigning the page.

## Purpose

This board assigns the closest suitable photographs already available in the Joshua's Point
repository to the approved Home content roles. It supports visual development only. It does not
create captions, identify unverified geography, invent a time of day, or claim that a photograph
shows an experience it does not record.

The approved visual hierarchy is:

```text
Joshua's Point
→ nature around the house
→ shared life
→ morning rhythm
→ Southern Negros
→ quiet return to Joshua's Point
```
