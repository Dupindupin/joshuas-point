### Nature experience

**Owner direction:** Lead with the jungle, landscape, walk, quiet, and waterfall. The nature
experience is inseparable from the journey to the falls.

Record what part of nature should lead the story—for example, only if Tobias confirms it, the
forest, water, rock, sound, weather, or the transition through the landscape.

**Editorial limit:** Do not add wildlife, ecological, geological, seasonal, or environmental
claims that Tobias has not provided and that have not been separately verified.

### Adventure level

**Owner direction:** Adventure is part of the experience and should remain visible in the
editorial positioning.

This records how Tobias wants the story framed, not an objective safety or difficulty rating.

**Allowed owner framing:** A nature-led journey with an element of adventure, discovery, and fun.

**Practical difficulty classification:** Requires separate verification and must not be inferred
from the owner framing.

### Ideal guest type

**Owner direction:** Tobias considers the experience best suited to guests with some fitness
because of the remembered steps down and climb back up.

**Source status:** Owner recommendation only. Fitness requirements, step count, difficulty, and
visitor suitability require verification before public wording is prepared.

Record who Tobias believes may appreciate the experience and why.

**Editorial limit:** Do not imply suitability for children, older visitors, disabled visitors,
swimmers, hikers, beginners, or any fitness level without separately verified access information.

### CASARORO-OWNER-008 — Intended recommendation style

Choose the closest editorial position. This is a content-framing decision, not a difficulty or
safety classification.

| Option | Meaning in this record | Owner choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relaxed nature day trip | A slower visit led by observation and time outdoors; does **not** claim easy access | Not selected |
| Adventure destination | The physical journey is central; requires careful verification to avoid unsupported difficulty or safety language | Supporting aspect |
| Scenic exploration | Landscape, approach, and visual experience lead; does **not** guarantee views or photography conditions | Supporting aspect |

**Owner choice:** “A hidden place to discover.”

**Owner reasoning:** The place is experienced through nature, adventure, discovery, the walk, and
the journey before and after reaching the waterfall.

**Editorial consequence:** Preserve the owner's sense of discovery without automatically using
“hidden” as final public wording. The phrase must not imply secrecy, restricted access, remoteness,
or exclusivity. Nature and journey should lead; adventure remains present but should not become a
difficulty claim.

### Positioning guardrails

Regardless of the selected option:

- record “hidden place to discover” and “must-see waterfall” as Tobias's source language, but do
  not assume those phrases are final public copy or use them to imply exclusivity or verified
  access;
- do not add unsupported “untouched,” “exclusive,” “spectacular,” or “unforgettable” language;
- do not turn physical effort into a challenge badge;
- do not describe access as relaxed, easy, hard, or safe without current verification;
- do not promise quiet, swimming, wildlife, weather, light, or water flow; and
- keep nature and the specific place ahead of activity marketing.
