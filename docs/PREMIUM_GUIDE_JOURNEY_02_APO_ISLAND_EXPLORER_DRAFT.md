## Editorial note

This prototype tests the Premium Guide format for an ocean and island day. It uses the existing
approved Joshua's Point content for orientation, relationships, responsible behavior, and the
general character of Apo Island.

It does not write Tobias's memories for him. Every part that requires his personal experience,
preference, or emotional observation is marked as owner input. It also avoids prices, schedules,
boat arrangements, technical dive information, and promises about marine life or conditions.

The intended result is a journey Tobias can complete in his own words—not a finished article built
from generic island language.

## 8. Free website versus Premium value boundary

The public platform must remain useful for understanding Apo Island, the gateway, responsible
behavior, and current practical corrections. The Premium Guide adds the considered day.

### Free website

Keep publicly available:

- Malatapay as the approved boat-departure gateway.

- Essential access, closure, conservation, conduct, and safety updates.
- Current questions travelers must confirm with a qualified local source.
- A representative, authentic reason Joshua's Point recommends the island.

### Premium journey

The paid guide should provide:

- Tobias's complete personal sequence from Joshua's Point and back.
- His reason for choosing Apo Island on a particular day.
- Meaningful transitions through Malatapay, the crossing, island, water, and return.
- Different day shapes for divers, snorkelers, and people staying mainly on land—only after owner
  and qualified review.
- Consolidated preparation and confirmation questions.
- An offline journey map and accessible text sequence.
- A truthful photographic essay joining mainland, crossing, island, water, community, and return.
- Edition-specific sources, credits, map version, and review date.

### Boundary test

The premium value must come from Tobias's selection, sequence, personal context, offline
orientation, and saved planning time. It must not come from withholding current crossing,
conservation, conduct, or safety information.

## 12. What must not be claimed publicly

Do not publish:

- Guaranteed turtles, wildlife, marine life, reef encounters, or photographic opportunities.
- Visibility, currents, depth, water temperature, dive level, entry type, or site conditions
  without qualified review.
- Technical dive-site coordinates.
- A promise that snorkeling, diving, swimming, equipment, guides, food, or facilities are
  available.
- Boat schedules, crossing times, fares, fees, registration, capacity, landing arrangements, or
  operator details unless current and explicitly approved.
- Guaranteed sea, weather, or crossing conditions.
- “Safe,” “easy,” “family-friendly,” or universal suitability claims.
- Guaranteed accessibility or mobility arrangements.
- Guaranteed turtle frequency or claims that animals can be approached closely.
- An operator ranking or recommendation Tobias has not approved.
- Claims that Joshua's Point operates, controls, or guarantees the crossing or island activity.
- Exact private Joshua's Point coordinates.
- A photograph as evidence of a place, species, activity, or condition unless its identity is
  confirmed.
- Private family stories, companions, or the private meaning of the Joshua name.
- Generic “paradise,” “hidden gem,” “world-class,” “unforgettable,” or luxury-island language.
