# Joshua's Point — Lake Balanan Migration Plan

## Status and purpose

This document applies the approved Destination Template to Lake Balanan using the faster migration
workflow:

```text
existing Joshua's Point content
→ focused owner input
→ claim and photography verification
→ no-index Sanity draft
```

It does not repeat the full House, Home, or Casaroro discovery process. It does not create Sanity
content, approve photography, or establish public facts.

Sources used:

- [`THE_EXISTING_DESTINATION_CONTENT_INVENTORY.md`](./THE_EXISTING_DESTINATION_CONTENT_INVENTORY.md)
- [`THE_DESTINATION_TEMPLATE_REVIEW.md`](./THE_DESTINATION_TEMPLATE_REVIEW.md)
- existing Joshua's Point page:
  [joshuaspoint.com/lake-balanan-siaton](https://joshuaspoint.com/lake-balanan-siaton/)
- related legacy sources identified in the inventory: Mountains & Lakes, Southern Explorer, and
  Siaton

## Migration principle

The existing Joshua's Point website already contains a useful Lake Balanan narrative seed. The
new destination should preserve its nature-first focus—water, forested hills, stillness, and a
slower pace—without treating the old site's activities, timing, access, or conditions as current
facts.

Existing content is a migration source, not automatic approval. Tobias's reason for recommending
the place must shape the draft before the legacy narrative is rewritten.

## 1. Existing source content worth preserving

### Existing editorial themes

The current page contains these potentially valuable themes:

- Morning stillness around the water.
- A lake held by forested hills.
- Time spent near or on the water.
- A bamboo-raft image and the visual rhythm of slow movement.
- Small waterfalls and forest paths as possible parts of the wider setting.
- A day shaped more by observation and pace than by collecting attractions.

These are `Legacy source candidates`. None should be presented as Tobias's observation until he
confirms it, and none of the associated activities should be presented as available until checked.

### Candidate narrative qualities

The source suggests a quieter rhythm than Casaroro Falls:

```text
approach and first view of the water
→ time at the lake
→ forest or surrounding landscape
→ a quiet departure or reflection
```

This is a candidate structure only. It must be revised around Tobias's real experience rather than
copied as a formula or padded with generic lake writing.

### Source-to-template mapping

| Destination role | Existing source material | Migration treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Water, forested hills, stillness | Preserve as direction; final wording needs owner source and a verified image |
| Introduction | Lake as a slower nature experience | Candidate; confirm why Joshua's Point recommends it |
| Experience/story | Arrival, water, raft, paths, small waterfalls, time outdoors | Determine which elements Tobias actually observed; remove unsupported activities |
| Why visit | Calm nature-led contrast to other regional experiences | Owner decision required; do not promise quiet or calm conditions |
| What to expect | Water, forest, paths, possible activities | Keep empty until current observation and practical verification |
| Photography notes | Morning stillness and reflections | Keep empty until light and access are observed and approved |
| Nearby context | Siaton, Najandig Peak, Southern Explorer | Relationship candidates only; do not imply a workable itinerary |

### Content not worth preserving verbatim

- Generic “hidden,” “perfect,” “rewarding,” or superlative framing.
- Promises of calm water, quiet, easy access, or a family-friendly visit.
- Activity lists presented without current availability.
- Suggested schedules and route combinations.
- Any sentence whose only purpose is to sell a day trip.

## 2. Focused owner input needed

The Lake Balanan owner pass should be short. It needs only enough source material to establish the
reason, story, and public boundaries.

### Joshua's Point recommendation

Record from Tobias:

- Why Joshua's Point recommends Lake Balanan.
- What he personally values about the place.
- What makes it fit the Joshua's Point nature-first approach.
- What he hopes guests notice, without promising a reaction.

### First-hand experience

Confirm:

- Whether Tobias has personally visited.
- What he clearly remembers without relying on the existing website.
- Which moment matters most: arrival, water, raft, forest, walking, weather, departure, or another
  detail he supplies.
- Which observations may support public editorial content.
- Whether any memory or person must remain private.

### Editorial positioning

Choose the owner's intended emphasis:

- quiet water and landscape;
- slow nature experience;
- time on or near the lake;
- forest exploration;
- shared day outdoors; or
- another owner-defined direction.

This is an editorial position, not evidence of weather, access, activities, safety, or visitor
suitability.

### Scope decisions

Tobias should decide:

- whether the destination story is primarily about the lake itself or the wider lake-and-forest
  setting;
- whether bamboo raft, boating, kayaking, swimming, waterfalls, and paths belong in the story only
  after verification;
- whether Siaton and Najandig are meaningful editorial relationships; and
- whether the Southern Explorer route should remain a later route candidate rather than part of
  the destination draft.

### Photography input

For each legacy image, record:

- whether Tobias recognizes it as Lake Balanan;
- owner or photographer;
- public credit requirement;
- rights for website and social use;
- consent for identifiable people;
- development-only or production status; and
- whether an original-resolution file is available.

No external research question is required during the owner-source pass. Operational facts remain
blocked for a later verification record.

## 6. SEO migration direction

### Search intent to preserve

The final page should identify Lake Balanan plainly and explain the specific value of the Joshua's
Point guide. SEO should emerge from the approved story and verified practical content rather than
from a keyword list.

### Prepared field direction

| SEO field | Migration direction | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `metaTitle` | Candidate structure: `Lake Balanan | Joshua's Point` | Confirm final title strategy |
| `metaDescription` | Write after Tobias's recommendation and the verified page scope exist | Must describe only content actually present |
| `socialTitle` | Inherit unless a distinct editorial reason exists | Optional |
| `socialDescription` | Inherit or remain empty | No unsupported activity or condition claims |
| `socialImage` | Empty | Add only after production rights and crop review |
| `canonicalUrl` | Empty during migration | Decide alongside old WordPress redirect strategy |
| `noIndex` | `true` in the first draft | Remove only at final publication approval |

### SEO claims to avoid

- Hidden gem, paradise, untouched, must-see, perfect, easiest, or best.
- Guaranteed calm water, quiet, reflections, weather, or views.
- Boating, kayaking, swimming, raft, waterfall, trail, or family claims before verification.
- Distance, time, route, fee, hours, or access promises.
- Generic lists of regional attractions added only for internal-link keywords.

The legacy URL should be preserved in the migration record. Redirect and canonical behavior must
be decided before launch, not guessed in the draft.

## 8. Fast migration sequence

1. Collect one focused Tobias source response covering recommendation, personal visit, strongest
   memory, nature-first emphasis, suitable audience direction, and public boundaries.
2. Match each owner observation against the legacy source; preserve agreement and flag conflict
   without forcing a resolution through copywriting.
3. Confirm the identity, ownership, rights, credits, and consent status of the four image
   candidates.
4. Prepare an editorial-only, no-index Sanity draft from approved owner material.
5. Assign a field reviewer and verify all practical and map fields separately.
6. Complete SEO only after the actual public scope is known.
7. Review the result against the Destination Template before any publication decision.

The immediate next step is the focused owner source record. Sanity entry should wait until that
input establishes why Lake Balanan belongs in the Joshua's Point guide.
