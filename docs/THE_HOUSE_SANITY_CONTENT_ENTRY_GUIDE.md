# Joshua's Point — The House Sanity Content Entry Guide

**Document type:** Internal editorial operations guide
**Sanity document:** `housePage` singleton
**Route supported:** `/the-house`
**Status:** Content-entry guidance; not public website copy

## Purpose

This guide explains how to populate the fixed `housePage` document without turning Sanity into a
place for speculative writing. It translates the approved House editorial records into practical
entry guidance for editors.

The source documents remain authoritative:

- `JOSHUA_POINT_HOUSE_SOUL.md`
- `THE_HOUSE_CONTENT_MAP.md`
- `THE_HOUSE_VERIFICATION_RECORD.md`
- `THE_HOUSE_SELECTED_PHOTOGRAPHY.md`
- `THE_HOUSE_PRE_SANITY_CHECKLIST.md`

This guide does not approve final copy, facts, captions, or photography. Owner-confirmed source is
not automatically publication-ready language.

## Entry principles

1. Open **Website → The House** in Studio. Do not create another House document.
2. Keep `workflowStatus` as **Draft** while copy, facts, rights, or photography are incomplete.
3. Enter only wording that has completed public editorial review. Do not paste planning copy,
   interviews, private memories, or verification records into public fields.
4. Use the existing image fields for approved production assets only. Current local photographs are
   development assets and are not final.
5. Leave optional sections or moments empty when their source is incomplete. Empty is preferable to
   plausible filler.
6. Complete `lastReviewedAt` only after a material factual and privacy review—not after a copy edit.
7. Move the workflow to **Approved** only after the final checklist in this guide is complete.

## Status language used here

| Status               | Meaning                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Available source** | Owner-confirmed information may support future writing, subject to the stated limits.                 |
| **Missing**          | Owner input, approved writing, photography, verification, rights, or another required item is absent. |
| **Excluded**         | The information must not enter the public document.                                                   |
| **Development only** | Useful for testing layout, but not approved for production publication.                               |

## Privacy boundary for the entire document

Never enter the following into public section fields, captions, alt text, SEO, previews, or image
metadata:

- the private origin or meaning of the Joshua's Point name;
- private information about Tobias, his wife, or other family relationships;
- private family memories, conversations, circumstances, objects, or routines;
- guest identities, stories, or images without explicit approval and consent;
- raw owner answers, interview transcripts, verification evidence, consent records, or rights
  agreements;
- precise access, coordinate, security, key, control, or system-operation details;
- unverified technical, material, geographical, environmental, or performance claims.

Public-safe architectural context may describe a shared vision between owner and architect. Do not
add private collaboration history. Preferred public credits and roles must be approved before the
names are published.

## Governance fields

### Workflow status

- **Draft:** default while any retained section is incomplete.
- **In Review:** public wording, facts, privacy, rights, and image presentation are ready for formal
  review.
- **Approved:** review is complete. This does not itself publish the document.

### Last reviewed date

Set `lastReviewedAt` only after the material facts, privacy boundaries, photography context, and
rights status have been reviewed. Do not update it for proofreading alone. It is required before
the document may be marked Approved.

### View verification notes

Use `view.verificationNotes` only for a concise, non-sensitive editorial status such as which
landmarks were checked and when. It is deliberately omitted from the frontend query, but it remains
stored in the Sanity dataset and therefore is not appropriate for secrets or private source.

## Final content-entry checklist

Before moving `housePage` to Approved:

- [ ] Every public sentence is approved and traceable to owner source or verified information.
- [ ] The private name origin and private family material are absent everywhere.
- [ ] Required Hero, Opening Reflection, Shared Heart, View, Indoor/Outdoor, and Closing content is
      complete.
- [ ] Optional Daily Rhythm moments contain both approved observation and approved photography.
- [ ] Materials and Architecture contains only verified entries; otherwise the section is absent.
- [ ] View landmarks are matched to the selected frame.
- [ ] Every image is a production original or approved derivative with rights, credit, crop,
      accessibility, privacy, and responsive review complete.
- [ ] Development photography has not been mistaken for final media.
- [ ] Meta description is complete and accurate.
- [ ] Studio-only verification notes contain no sensitive source or private information.
- [ ] `lastReviewedAt` reflects a material review.
- [ ] Workflow status is Approved only after editorial, factual, privacy, accessibility, and rights
      review.
