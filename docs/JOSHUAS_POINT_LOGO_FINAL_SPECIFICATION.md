# Joshua's Point — Final Logo Specification

**Status:** Approved identity direction and Version 1 asset specification
**Direction:** Option 2 — Joshua's Point Signature
**Variation:** Signature No. 5

## 1. Identity idea

The Joshua's Point logo is the signature of a place someone loves.

Its handwritten `JP` mark begins as a personal gesture and settles into a quiet horizontal line.
The movement suggests horizon, landscape, and the connection between sea and mountain without
turning either into a literal illustration. A single warm point gives the signature a sense of
place.

The identity is personal, warm, quiet, timeless, nature-connected, and humble. It frames the story
of Joshua's Point; it does not try to become the story.

### One coherent family

Signature No. 5 is the source mark for every asset. The compact mark, favicon, social profile, and
application icon use its pure JP-and-horizon form. In the primary and stacked Option 2 lockups, the
same uninterrupted horizon extends into the restrained mountain gesture from the approved
presentation. This is an expanded lockup of Signature No. 5, not a separate symbol or logo concept.

## 2. Logo family

### Primary horizontal logo

**File:** `logo-horizontal.svg`
**Canvas:** 1200 × 360
**Use:** Desktop header, wide editorial layouts, Open Graph compositions, title pages, and landscape
print formats.

The primary lockup contains the JP signature mark, the Joshua's Point wordmark, and the approved
tagline:

> A place between sea and mountain

The tagline may be omitted in applications where it becomes too small or where the surrounding
content already carries the thought. The mark and wordmark relationship must otherwise remain
unchanged.

### Stacked logo

**File:** `logo-stacked.svg`
**Canvas:** 640 × 720
**Use:** Mobile and narrow compositions, book title pages, portrait print formats, and restrained
centered applications.

### Compact mark

**File:** `logo-mark.svg`
**Canvas:** 512 × 512
**Use:** Small interface areas, social authorship, map attribution to Joshua's Point, and applications
where the full name already appears nearby.

The compact mark must not replace the full wordmark when the audience cannot otherwise identify
Joshua's Point.

### Light logo

**File:** `logo-light.svg`
**Canvas:** 1200 × 360
**Use:** Deep ocean, forest, charcoal, evening surfaces, and carefully selected photography overlays.

Photography must provide sufficient quiet space and contrast. Do not add a shadow, glow, or opaque
badge to force the logo onto a busy image.

### Dark logo

**File:** `logo-dark.svg`
**Canvas:** 1200 × 360
**Use:** Linen, warm sand, paper, light documents, and pale photographic areas.

### Favicon and application assets

- `favicon.svg` — 64 × 64 scalable browser icon.
- `social-profile.png` — 1080 × 1080 square with circular-crop safe space.
- `app-icon.png` — 1024 × 1024 square application master.

The compact assets use the same JP signature geometry with a heavier effective stroke at small size.
They do not attempt to reproduce the wordmark or tagline.

## 3. Clear space

Use the gold point in the signature as the clear-space unit, **P**.

- Primary and Light/Dark horizontal logos: keep at least **2P** clear on every side.
- Stacked logo: keep at least **2P** clear on every side.
- Compact mark: keep at least **1.5P** clear on every side.
- Social and application crops: the supplied assets already contain the minimum safe area; do not
  enlarge the mark within their canvases.

No text, border, photograph focal point, map control, or other brand mark should enter this area.

## 4. Minimum size

Minimum sizes preserve the signature stroke, apostrophe, and wordmark spacing.

| Asset                           | Screen minimum |  Print minimum |
| ------------------------------- | -------------: | -------------: |
| Horizontal logo with tagline    |    360 px wide |     90 mm wide |
| Horizontal logo without tagline |    260 px wide |     65 mm wide |
| Stacked logo with tagline       |    220 px wide |     55 mm wide |
| Compact mark                    |     40 px wide |     10 mm wide |
| Favicon                         |          16 px | Not applicable |

Below these sizes, use the compact mark or favicon. Never keep the tagline when it is no longer
comfortably legible.

## 5. Colour system

The logo palette comes from the approved identity reference.

| Colour     | Hex       | Role                                                       |
| ---------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deep Ocean | `#1F3D3A` | Primary signature mark, compact identity, deep backgrounds |
| Forest     | `#496B5B` | Restrained supporting applications                         |
| Warm Sand  | `#C8A26A` | Point and tagline accent                                   |
| Linen      | `#F3EDE6` | Light mark and warm background                             |
| Charcoal   | `#282828` | Wordmark and single-colour dark use                        |

Use Deep Ocean and Charcoal on Linen or similarly light backgrounds. Use Linen on Deep Ocean,
Forest, or approved dark surfaces. Warm Sand is an accent; it must not become the dominant logo
colour.

When production restrictions require one colour, reproduce the entire identity in either Charcoal
or Linen. Do not introduce gradients, metallic simulation, transparency effects, or unapproved
colours.

## 6. Typography

The wordmark uses **Newsreader Medium**, the official Joshua's Point display typeface, in restrained
capitals with custom spacing. This keeps the established identity while bringing its weight and
rhythm closer to the website's editorial headings. The tagline uses **Manrope**, the official body
and interface typeface.

The supplied wordmark SVGs contain embedded font data so the approved typography remains stable
when the files are used outside the website. Compact assets contain no text and therefore require no
embedded font. Do not retype the wordmark in another serif or reconstruct the spacing manually.

Always retain the apostrophe in **Joshua's Point**. The location is not part of the primary logo.
When required in separate brand material, use:

> Calango, Zamboanguita
> Negros Oriental, Philippines

Do not use Siaton as the Joshua's Point location.

## 7. Background and photographic use

- Prefer quiet, uncluttered areas with strong contrast.
- Keep natural photography unchanged; do not tint or darken an entire photograph merely to place
  the logo.
- Use the Light version over dark photography and the Dark version over pale photography.
- If neither version remains clearly legible, move the logo outside the image.
- Do not position the signature across faces, architecture details, mountain ridges, or other image
  focal points.

## 8. Incorrect usage

Never:

- Redraw, rotate, skew, stretch, condense, or rearrange the signature.
- Separate the gold point from the JP mark or move it to imply a conventional location pin.
- Replace the signature with typed initials.
- Change the relative scale of the mark, wordmark, and tagline.
- Use the wordmark without its apostrophe.
- Add palm trees, waves, resort symbols, roofs, crests, badges, or hotel stars.
- Add gradients, bevels, metallic effects, drop shadows, glows, outlines, or textures.
- Place the logo inside a decorative seal or holding shape.
- Use Deep Ocean on a similarly dark background or Linen on a similarly pale background.
- Place the detailed lockup below its minimum size.
- Use the location as a substitute tagline in the primary lockup.
- Describe Joshua's Point as being in Siaton.

## 9. File handling

- Treat the SVG files as the source assets for digital and scalable use.
- Preserve their view boxes, embedded fonts, accessible titles, and descriptions.
- Generate future raster sizes from the approved vectors rather than from screenshots.
- Keep master assets lossless and use optimised derivatives for delivery.
- Do not replace website wordmarks until the separate implementation step is approved.

## 10. Approval test

Before using the identity, confirm:

1. The correct lockup and Light/Dark version are selected.
2. Clear space and minimum size are respected.
3. The full name and apostrophe are legible.
4. The logo supports rather than competes with photography.
5. The result feels like a personal signature of place—not a hotel brand.
