## Purpose

This record separates approved editorial principles from claims that require evidence before they
appear in House copy, captions, metadata, alt text, or structured content. It is a working
checklist, not a source of public prose.

Private family information, the private origin of the Joshua's Point name, and unpublished owner
memories remain outside this record. A source reference should identify evidence without copying
private material into a public-content workflow.

## Status vocabulary

- **Principle only:** approved as editorial direction, not verified as a physical or technical fact.
- **Unverified:** suggested or previously implied, but no accepted evidence is recorded.
- **Not recorded:** no claim should be made until the underlying information is supplied.
- **Pending confirmation:** a likely source is known, but confirmation has not been recorded.
- **Needs terminology review:** the owner-confirmed subject is present, but its technically correct
  public name has not been established.
- **Owner-confirmed:** recorded directly from Tobias Steger as owner source. This is sufficient for a
  personal observation; technical, legal, dimensional, geographical, and performance claims may
  still require documentary or specialist confirmation.
- **Verified:** evidence, approver, scope, and verification date have been recorded.
- **Excluded:** intentionally not for public use, even if internally known.

To mark an item **Verified**, replace its status and complete the final column with the source,
confirming person, confirmation date, and any limits on wording or publication. Owner recollection
may verify a personal observation; technical, geographical, legal, dimensional, and performance
claims may require additional records or specialist confirmation.

## Publication gate

Before House content or photography is treated as public-ready:

- Every factual sentence, caption, alt-text fact, metadata claim, and credit has a verification ID
  or is removed.
- Personal observations are clearly distinguished from architectural or performance facts.
- Design intent is attributed only with architect/owner evidence.
- Dimensions use an identified measurement basis.
- Material identities and sourcing claims have records.
- Environmental claims have a defined basis and do not exceed the evidence.
- Image rights, edits, credits, consent, and privacy review are complete.
- Private family information and the origin of the name remain excluded.
- An owner approves the public scope and records the approval date.
