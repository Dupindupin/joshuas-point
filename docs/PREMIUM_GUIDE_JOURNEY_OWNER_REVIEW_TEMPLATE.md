# Joshua's Point — Premium Guide Journey Owner Review Template

## Purpose

Use this record before writing or implementing any Premium Guide journey. It collects the owner
source, practical boundaries, photography needs, and map needs required to turn canonical Joshua's
Point content into a considered day.

The template is based on
[`PREMIUM_GUIDE_TEST_JOURNEY_WATERFALL_EXPLORER.md`](./PREMIUM_GUIDE_TEST_JOURNEY_WATERFALL_EXPLORER.md).
It is an internal source and approval record—not final guide copy.

The journey should sound like Tobias quietly showing someone a place he knows and loves. It should
never sound like a hotel, tour operator, destination ranking, or generic travel blog.

## Source-status vocabulary

Use one status for every recorded statement.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner observation** | Something Tobias personally experienced, noticed, or remembers |
| **Owner recommendation** | Tobias's reason for sharing a place or journey |
| **Owner editorial choice** | A decision about emphasis, sequence, audience, or tone—not proof of a fact |
| **Verified canonical fact** | Information held by the approved destination, route, dive, or map source |
| **Changing information** | Access, fees, opening, weather, transport, conditions, or another detail requiring current confirmation |
| **Needs qualified review** | Specialist information, including technical dive or safety guidance |
| **Do not publish** | Private, unsupported, misleading, rights-restricted, or explicitly excluded material |

### Recording rules

- Preserve Tobias's meaning and natural language.
- Record “I do not know,” “I do not remember,” and “leave this out” without filling the gap.
- Do not turn one personal experience into a universal promise.
- Do not infer facts from a map estimate, photograph, memory, or marketing source.
- Reference canonical destinations, scenic routes, dive guides, relationships, and maps. Do not
  duplicate their factual fields in the journey.
- Keep private family history and the private meaning of the Joshua name excluded.
- Essential corrections, closures, conservation rules, and safety information must remain public.

## Final editorial test

Before drafting final guide text, ask:

1. Could this journey have been written without Tobias's knowledge? If yes, the source is too
   generic.
2. Does every emotional detail come from a real observation? If no, remove it.
3. Does every practical claim have a canonical or reviewed source? If no, leave it empty.
4. Does the sequence help someone choose and understand the day? If no, it is still a list.
5. Would the free website remain useful without the premium guide? If no, correct the boundary.
6. Does the journey feel like Tobias quietly sharing a place he loves? If no, return to the owner
   source before writing more.
