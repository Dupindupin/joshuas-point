# Southern Negros Premium Guide — Edition 1 Production Structure

## Status

This is the production structure for Edition 1. It converts the approved editorial blueprint into
one governed publication plan without creating final PDF layouts, frontend work, Sanity schemas, or
new destination copy.

The guide remains centered on one question:

> What should guests discover while staying at Joshua's Point?

Edition 1 contains nine chapters and five core journeys. Three journeys have approved editorial
foundations. Two require Tobias's source record before drafting.

## Editorial authority

- Source work begins with `https://guide.joshuaspoint.com/` for existing destination information,
  practical guidance, local background, and editorial text.
- The existing Joshua's Point website follows as the source for destinations, scenic routes, dive
  guides, photography references, maps, and content relationships.
- Approved Tobias material provides the personal experience, local perspective, recommendation,
  and premium way of shaping the day.
- Verified public sources may fill remaining history, nature, culture, or geography gaps only.
  They may never be used to infer Tobias's memories, visits, feelings, opinions, or recommendations.
- Canonical place facts remain in `destination` documents.
- Verified paths and ordered stops remain in `scenicRoute` documents.
- Technical and specialist-reviewed marine information remains in `diveSite` documents.
- Coordinates remain in existing map objects and route geometry.
- Related content is resolved through the Relationship Engine.
- Premium chapters own selection, sequence, Tobias's perspective, planning knowledge, and the
  relationship between otherwise separate public records.
- The public Joshua's Point website remains the source for current corrections and essential
  access or safety information.

The guide must never copy canonical records into a second place, route, dive, image, or coordinate
system.

For each chapter, editors must separately record: The Place, Why We Recommend It, The Journey,
Local Perspective, Practical Guidance, and Premium Recommendations. Existing source material is
preserved and improved before any new passage is drafted.

## Chapter production records

### Chapter 1 — From Joshua's Point

**Purpose:** Establish Joshua's Point as the emotional and practical center of the guide.

**Uses:** Approved House and Home material; Getting Here and Plan Your Stay practical information;
public-safe location context; the five journey records below.

**Premium contribution:** A journey chooser based on the character of the day, preparation, energy,
and interests rather than rankings.

**Maps and relationships:** One public-safe regional orientation linking Joshua's Point to the five
journeys. Do not expose a private property coordinate.

**Status:** Structure ready. Tobias's short opening note and his way of choosing a day remain owner
inputs; these are chapter inputs, not additional journeys.

### Chapter 2 — Arriving through Dumaguete

**Purpose:** Help a reader arrive calmly and understand the gateway before traveling south.

**Uses:** Existing Getting Here content, verified practical notes, Dumaguete destination context,
and the canonical `coastal-ride-to-dumaguete` scenic route where useful.

**Premium contribution:** A maintained arrival sequence and confirmation checklist. This chapter is
not a sixth journey and should not duplicate the public Getting Here page.

**Maps and relationships:** Airport, port, restrained city orientation, and the southbound
relationship to Joshua's Point.

**Status:** Chapter structure ready. Current practical recommendations and review dates remain
required before production copy.

### Chapter 3 — Zamboanguita and the coast around home

**Purpose:** Show that meaningful discovery can begin close to Joshua's Point.

**Uses:** Existing Zamboanguita destination context, Malatapay content, the Apo Island gateway
relationship, the Zamboanguita dive-area record for general orientation only, and approved owner
photography.

**Premium contribution:** Journey 04, a nearby morning or afternoon shaped by coast, local context,
and time rather than a collection of attractions.

**Maps and relationships:** General Zamboanguita orientation, Malatapay gateway, and public-safe
Joshua's Point context. No technical dive coordinates.

**Status:** Missing journey. Owner review required before editorial drafting.

### Chapter 4 — Dauin and the marine coast

**Purpose:** Present Dauin as a coast and marine landscape, not a directory of dive operators.

**Uses:** Existing Dauin destination, Dauin dive-area content, marine-sanctuary material after
qualified review, and current practical recommendations only where Tobias still supports them.

**Premium contribution:** Journey 05, one considered Dauin day that distinguishes coastal,
snorkeling, and diving possibilities without technical promises.

**Maps and relationships:** General Dauin area markers and accessible text orientation. Technical
dive-site coordinates remain outside this journey unless separately reviewed.

**Status:** Missing journey. Owner review and qualified boundaries required before editorial
drafting.

### Chapter 5 — Valencia and the highlands

**Purpose:** Carry the reader from the coast into forest, water, rock, and a more physical day.

**Uses:** Canonical Casaroro Falls and Pulangbato Falls destinations, the `waterfall-explorer`
scenic route, related Valencia context, approved owner observations, and existing map geometry.

**Premium contribution:** Journey 01 — **Waterfall Explorer Day**. The journey draft remains the
source for Tobias's experience; destination and route records remain authoritative for place facts,
markers, and route geometry.

**Maps and relationships:** The verified Waterfall Explorer route, Casaroro Falls, Pulangbato Falls,
and text sequence. The existing `valencia-highlands-loop` may support a shorter alternative but is
not a separate premium journey without an editorial need.

**Status:** Editorial foundation approved. Practical facts, final photography, credits, and review
dates remain production tasks.

### Chapter 6 — Siaton: lake, forest, and sheltered coast

**Purpose:** Connect the road south with mountain landscape, lake, forest, and sheltered coast.

**Uses:** Canonical Lake Balanan content, the `southern-explorer` scenic route, future approved
Tambobo Bay material, and existing route relationships.

**Premium contribution:** Journey 03 — **Mountain & Lake Explorer Day**. Tobias's observations are
approved, but the draft deliberately leaves the exact route and primary lake unresolved.

**Maps and relationships:** No premium map should be released until Tobias confirms whether Journey
03 maps to the existing Southern Explorer route and Lake Balanan. Once confirmed, reuse its verified
geometry and stops rather than redrawing them.

**Status:** Editorial foundation approved; route identity, primary lake, optional water stop, and
practical boundaries remain owner decisions. It is not counted as an additional missing journey.

### Chapter 7 — Apo Island

**Purpose:** Give the island journey enough space for anticipation, gateway, crossing, island,
water, community, and return.

**Uses:** Canonical Apo Island destination, Malatapay gateway, general-area map marker, Apo Island
dive-guide relationship, and approved owner observations.

**Premium contribution:** Journey 02 — **Apo Island Explorer Day**. Its final editorial draft is the
source for Tobias's perspective. Diving remains distinct from snorkeling, and technical claims stay
with specialist-reviewed dive content.

**Maps and relationships:** Land relationship to Malatapay, an editorial—not navigational—sea
connection, and the verified general Apo Island marker. No exact boat track or technical dive-site
coordinate.

**Status:** Editorial foundation approved. Changeable practical information, final photography,
credits, and review dates remain production tasks.

### Chapter 8 — Roads worth taking

**Purpose:** Explain the pleasure and practical logic of the road without duplicating route pages.

**Uses:** Canonical Edition 1 route records and their stops, geometry, related destinations, and
reviewed practical notes.

**Edition 1 route set:**

- `waterfall-explorer` — supports Journey 01.
- `southern-explorer` — intended to support Journey 03 after Tobias confirms the match.
- `coastal-ride-to-dumaguete` — supports Chapter 2 and may inform Chapter 4.
- `valencia-highlands-loop` — a possible shorter alternative inside Chapter 5.

`twin-lakes-escape` remains outside Edition 1 under the approved blueprint.

**Premium contribution:** Comparison, selection, shorter alternatives, and offline map assembly.
The guide references route stories and geometry; it does not rewrite them.

**Status:** Structure ready. The final route order, Journey 03 route match, and current practical
review remain owner decisions.

### Chapter 9 — Practical field notes

**Purpose:** Put changeable, useful preparation in one maintained place without turning the guide
into a directory.

**Uses:** Existing Plan Your Stay, Getting Here, FAQ, amenities, destination travel information,
scenic-route guidance, and public correction links.

**Premium contribution:** A concise offline confirmation framework organized by need, with source
and review dates.

**Maps and relationships:** Area index and links back to canonical public pages. No duplicated
coordinates or unmaintained provider lists.

**Status:** Structure ready. Current provider recommendations, emergency orientation, and review
ownership require confirmation before release.

## Production boundaries

- No prices, schedules, fees, journey times, access conditions, or provider recommendations are
  added without current source review.
- No technical dive data is moved into premium journey prose.
- No exact private Joshua's Point coordinate is used.
- Development photography remains replaceable and clearly marked until approved for release.
- A missing optional section disappears; it is not filled with generic travel writing.
- Essential corrections, conservation notices, and safety-relevant changes remain available on the
  public website.
- The premium value is the curated sequence and Tobias's perspective, not withholding basic facts.
