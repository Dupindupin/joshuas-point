# Joshua's Point — Color System

Version: 1.0 (Living Document)

---

# Philosophy

The colors of Joshua's Point are not chosen from design trends.

They are discovered in the place itself.

Every color should remind visitors of something they will actually experience:

- warm timber beneath their feet
- soft linen inside the house
- the changing sea
- the surrounding forest
- evening light
- quiet mornings

The interface should never compete with nature.

It should frame it.

---

# Primary Palette

## Warm Linen

HEX: #F6F2EB

Inspired by:

Morning light entering the house.

Purpose:

Primary page background.

Feeling:

Warm
Quiet
Inviting

Use for:

- Main backgrounds
- Reading sections
- Forms
- Long content

Avoid:

- Buttons
- Warnings

---

## Deep Charcoal

HEX: #242424

Inspired by:

Window frames and evening shadows.

Purpose:

Primary text.

Feeling:

Elegant
Architectural
Calm

Use for:

- Headlines
- Navigation
- Body text
- Footer

Avoid:

Large background areas in Day mode.

---

# Secondary Palette

## Warm Timber

HEX: #A56F3A

Inspired by:

The timber throughout Joshua's Point.

Purpose:

Brand accent.

Feeling:

Natural
Warm
Crafted

Use for:

- Buttons
- Links
- Hover states
- Highlights

Never overuse.

It should feel special.

---

## Stone

HEX: #B7B0A6

Inspired by:

Concrete and natural stone.

Purpose:

Supporting neutral.

Use for:

- Borders
- Cards
- Dividers
- Secondary backgrounds

---

## Forest

HEX: #405548

Inspired by:

The surrounding landscape.

Purpose:

Natural accent.

Use sparingly.

Never dominate.

---

## Ocean

HEX: #6A8EA1

Inspired by:

The calm sea viewed from the deck.

Purpose:

Secondary accent.

# Rules

✓ Nature is always more colorful than the interface.

✓ The website should never look tropical.

✓ Avoid bright saturated colors.

✓ White is never pure white.

✓ Black is never pure black.

✓ Warmth is more important than contrast.

✓ Color supports photography.

Photography never supports color.
