# Joshua's Point — Day & Evening

Version: 1.0 (Living Document)

---

# Philosophy

Joshua's Point is beautiful at every hour.

The website should reflect this.

We do not simply offer a Light Mode and a Dark Mode.

We offer two different atmospheres.

Morning and Evening.

---

# Morning

Feeling

Fresh.

Open.

Natural.

Hopeful.

Inspired by

Morning coffee on the deck.

Soft sunlight.

Sea breeze.

Birdsong.

Colors

Warm Linen

Soft Timber

Ocean Blue

Natural Green

Photography

Bright but never overexposed.

Warm shadows.

Natural light.

Movement

Gentle.

Slow.

Fresh.

---

# Evening

Feeling

Quiet.

Warm.

Reflective.

Comfortable.

Inspired by

Dinner on the deck.

Pool lights.

Warm timber.

Stars.

Silence.

Colors

Deep Charcoal

Warm Ivory

Soft Copper

Muted Ocean

Photography

Warm interior lighting.

Pool reflections.

Blue hour.

Night atmosphere.

Movement

Even slower.

More cinematic.

Calm fades.

Soft transitions.

---

# Theme Switching

The transition between Morning and Evening should feel like sunset.

Not like switching software themes.

Animations should be slow.

Natural.

Elegant.

---

# Rules

Never use pure white.

Never use pure black.

Warmth is more important than brightness.

Every theme should feel like Joshua's Point.

Not like a computer setting.

---

# Accessibility

Both themes must maintain excellent readability.

Contrast should always meet accessibility standards.

Comfort comes before aesthetics.

---

# Remember

The website should feel alive.

Just like Joshua's Point changes throughout the day.
