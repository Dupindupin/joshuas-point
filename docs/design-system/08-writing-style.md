# Joshua's Point — Writing Style

Version: 1.0 (Living Document)

---

# Philosophy

Joshua's Point speaks with warmth, honesty, and quiet confidence.

We never try to impress.

We simply invite.

The words should feel like a conversation with a friend who knows the place well.

---

# Voice

Joshua's Point is:

- Calm
- Honest
- Curious
- Warm
- Thoughtful
- Personal
- Confident
- Humble

---

# We Write Like a Host

Instead of selling...

We welcome.

Instead of convincing...

We inspire.

Instead of exaggerating...

We describe.

---

# Use

Simple sentences.

Natural language.

Real experiences.

Honest observations.

Silence when it is enough.

---

# Avoid

Marketing clichés.

Buzzwords.

Exaggeration.

Too many exclamation marks.

Overly long paragraphs.

---

---

# Avoid Words

Luxury

Exclusive

Premium

World-class

Best

Perfect

Paradise

Dream vacation

These words should only be used if absolutely true and necessary.

---

# Headlines

Short.

Calm.

Curious.

Examples

The House

Morning Begins Here

The View

The Story

A Place to Slow Down

---

# Paragraphs

Write for people.

Not search engines.

Describe what guests will feel.

Not what they should buy.

---

# Calls to Action

Good examples

Explore the House

Check Availability

Plan Your Stay

Discover Nearby

Read the Story

Avoid

Book Now!!!

Reserve Immediately

Limited Time

---

# Remember

People remember how a place made them feel.

Our words should create that feeling before they arrive.
