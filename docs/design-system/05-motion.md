# Joshua's Point — Motion

Version: 1.0 (Living Document)

---

# Philosophy

Motion should never attract attention.

Motion should support the story.

Visitors should feel the website breathing.

Never rushing.

Never shouting.

---

# Feeling

Imagine sitting on the deck.

The wind moves slowly.

Clouds drift across the mountains.

Water reflects the light.

That is our animation style.

---

# Motion Principles

Slow.

Natural.

Elegant.

Calm.

Confident.

Meaningful.

---

# Timing

Micro interactions

150–250ms

Small UI feedback.

Buttons.

Hover states.

Navigation.

---

Content Reveal

600–900ms

Images.

Headlines.

Paragraphs.

---

Large Scene Changes

1000–1500ms

Hero transitions.

Section changes.

Theme changes.

---

# Animation Types

Fade

Primary animation.

Slide

Only small distances.

Parallax

Very subtle.

Scale

Maximum 1.02

Blur

Minimal.

---

# Never

Bounce.

Flash.

Spin.

Shake.

Large zooms.

Fast movement.

Everything should feel timeless.

---

# Scroll

Scrolling should feel like walking through the property.

Not like flipping pages.

Each section should reveal itself naturally.

---

# Day & Evening

Transitions between Day and Evening should feel like sunset.

Not like flipping a switch.

---

# Sound

Motion should work beautifully even without sound.

Silence is part of the experience.

---

# Remember

Visitors should remember the place.

Not the animations.
