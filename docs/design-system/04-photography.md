# Joshua's Point — Photography

Version: 1.0 (Living Document)

---

# Philosophy

Photography is the heart of Joshua's Point.

People do not fall in love with floor plans.

They fall in love with moments.

Every photograph should make the visitor imagine being there.

---

# The Goal

Do not document the property.

Tell the story of life at Joshua's Point.

Every image should answer one question:

"What would it feel like to be here?"

---

# Our Heroes

Photography should always focus on one of these:

1. Nature
2. Architecture
3. People
4. Light

---

# Nature

Capture:

- Morning mist
- Rain
- Clouds
- Birds
- Butterflies
- Trees
- Mountains
- Sea
- Flowers

Nature is never decoration.

Nature is the main character.

---

# Architecture

Show:

- Long roof lines
- Timber
- Shadows
- Texture
- Open spaces
- Framing
- Windows
- Deck

Never try to make the house look bigger.

Make it feel welcoming.

---

# People

Never use fake models.

Use real moments.

Examples

Morning coffee.

Cooking together.

Reading.

Swimming.

Looking at the mountains.

Talking around the dining table.

Watching the sunset.

Laughing.

Silence.

---

# Light

Light tells the story.

Photograph:

Sunrise.

Golden hour.

Blue hour.

Rain.

Night.

Pool reflections.

Warm interior lighting.

Morning shadows.

The changing light is part of Joshua's Point.

---

# Composition

Use generous negative space.

Let architecture breathe.

Avoid clutter.

Avoid over-editing.

Do not oversaturate colors.

Keep the atmosphere natural.

---

# Details

Look for small moments.

Coffee cups.

Wet towels.

Open books.

Wood texture.

Rain on glass.

Reflections.

Plants moving in the wind.

These details create emotion.

---

# Drone

Drone footage should show:

The relationship between the house and nature.

Not simply altitude.

Fly slowly.

Use long movements.

Avoid dramatic fast rotations.

---

# Video

Videos should feel cinematic.

Natural sound is preferred.

Birds.

Wind.

Rain.

Water.

Silence.

Music should never overpower nature.

---

# Editing

Warm.

Natural.

Timeless.

Avoid heavy filters.

Avoid trendy editing styles.

Joshua's Point should still look beautiful in ten years.

---

# Never Photograph

Mess.

Artificial poses.

Oversaturated skies.

Harsh HDR.

Crowded compositions.

Anything that feels forced.

---

# Remember

We are not photographing a building.

We are photographing memories waiting to happen.
