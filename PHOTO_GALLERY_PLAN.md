# Joshua’s Point Photography Gallery Plan

## Purpose

This plan establishes one photography system for the Joshua’s Point platform and the Southern Negros Premium Guide. It covers every existing Sanity document of type `destination`, `diveSite`, and `scenicRoute` in the production dataset as reviewed on 12 August 2026.

No Sanity content has been changed. Proposed captions are editorial directions, not approved final captions. They must be matched to the selected photograph and checked for factual accuracy before publication.

## One editorial photography system

### Hero image

- One unmistakable, place-specific opening image.
- Prefer a horizontal image between 3:2 and 16:9 with enough crop tolerance for desktop and mobile.
- The hero must identify the page’s central subject. Route heroes should show the road or journey, not merely one endpoint.
- Do not reuse the same hero across a destination, its parent region, and its scenic route once dedicated photography is available.

### Editorial gallery

Each gallery should read as a short photo essay rather than a thumbnail collection. The preferred sequence is:

1. **Orientation** — where the reader has arrived.
2. **Approach** — road, path, crossing, or first transition.
3. **Immersion** — the defining landscape, water, forest, town, or underwater environment.
4. **Observed detail** — material, wildlife, working activity, or a small local detail.
5. **Human scale** — a truthful, consented moment that shows how the place is experienced.
6. **Return or wider context** — the landscape opening again or the journey continuing.

Four strong photographs are better than eight repetitive ones. Most pages should use five to seven images. Dive and route stories may use two named sequences where the above structure cannot express the full experience.

### Captions

- State what the photograph actually shows and why that moment matters.
- Keep captions to one sentence where possible.
- Separate direct observation from changing practical information.
- Never imply guaranteed wildlife, weather, access, activity availability, or crowd levels.
- Do not identify people, villages, private land, or sensitive sites without permission.
- Every image also needs factual alt text, credit, rights status, orientation, and a reviewed focal point.

### Cross-platform reuse

- Sanity remains the image authority.
- A destination or route may supply selected photography to the Premium Guide, but the Guide should reference the same asset rather than create a duplicate upload.
- One image may appear in related contexts when truthful, but it should not become the default visual for an entire region.
- Development photography must retain the label: **Development photography — not production approved — replace before launch.**

# Implementation guardrails for the later gallery phase

- Populate and approve the hero before assembling the gallery.
- Do not publish a gallery containing only repeated crops of the hero.
- Require alt text and credit on every image; require a caption unless the image is explicitly decorative.
- Keep image order editorially controlled in Sanity.
- Record focal point/hotspot for desktop, mobile, social, and print crops.
- Hide an empty gallery rather than showing reserved public UI on published destination pages.
- Premium Guide reserved-image treatments may remain during production review but should not appear in the paid release.
- Do not copy House or Home photography into destination galleries merely to fill space.
- Do not upload duplicate assets when the same approved Sanity image already exists.
